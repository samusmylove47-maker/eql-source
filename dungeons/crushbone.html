01Revamp status

Revamped 14 July 2026, then touched again on launch day. This is the most recently maintained low-level dungeon in the game.

The 14 July patch describes Clan Crushbone fortifying and multiplying its army while the elves of Faydark were distracted with politics — mechanically, fresh loot and new enemies across levels 4 through 22. Six named were added: Bloodgurgler, Bonefire, Chokehold, Marrowbane, Lord Darish and The Prophet, along with a whole attunable gear tier that did not exist in classic.

Launch day, 28 July 2026 — five further changes.
  • Rare monsters in Crushbone are now flagged as rare monsters.
  • Orc Scoutsman and Orc Warden respawn timers substantially reduced.
  • Chokehold is now susceptible to melee attacks — he was not before. Melee groups that bounced off him should try again.
  • Headsplitter Axe is no longer usable by Monks, Rogues or Beastlords. Older loot lists are wrong on this.
  • Spiky Splintmail's click has been shifted to Bramblecoat, and the item now has a worn graphic. Slave Trammels were also corrected to render as leg irons, several Crushbone items gained worn graphics, and ornamentation slots were added to the new model-visible drops.

03The camps

Levels 8–13

Trainer Hill

Six or seven orcs on the hilltop with a respawn fast enough that you never pull from elsewhere. The wiki calls it the place camping was invented.

Levels 5–8 · 12–16

Slaver Caves & Pit

Left on zone-in for the caves; follow the right wall for the pit. The safe spot is in front of the building by the tree. Killing slaves damages faction with their home city.

Levels 14–18

Wall

Directly in front of the bridge to the castle. Pull the orcs roaming outside, then from inside once the group can hold it.

Levels 17–22

Throne Room

Six or seven legionnaires and oracles spawn inside on a very fast timer, so it is hard to hold. A group that can hold it can pull Emperor Crush and Ambassador DVinn from the tower.

Levels 8–14

Tents

The tented area at the very back, holding orcs between pawn and legionnaire. Shares a four-spawn crevice with the Slaver cave. Corpse runs from here are unpleasant.

Turn-in experience. Orcs drop Crushbone Belts and Crushbone Shoulderpads, which hand in to Canloe Nusback (GM Warrior, Kaladim) for experience and cheap armour. Belts one at a time, shoulderpads two at a time. Belts stay worth doing to about level 10; shoulderpads well past the point most players leave the zone.

06Dangers

Trains every five to ten minutes. Orc spawns sit close together and the zone is busy. Expect legionnaires, oracles, an emissary or occasionally Emperor Crush himself to come through the zone line. Learn the paths trains follow and stay off them.
Pathing is broken in places. Be careful over bridges — especially toward the slaver camp and outside the keep where Wall pulls happen. If a pulled orc suddenly goes for a swim, its route out of the water runs behind the castle through everything else in the zone.
The Royal Guard is invisible until it attacks. Anyone pushing to Emperor Crush should assume it is there.
Faction costs. Killing slaves damages faction with their home city. Retlon Brenclog costs Ak'Anon faction.

07Getting there

  • One entrance, directly north of Kelethin in Greater Faydark. Take the northernmost lift — the one furthest from the other two — and head north, or follow the path straight out. The entrance is ringed by two pyres.
  • From Felwithe, turn right out of the main entrance and follow the zone border. Duller than the Kelethin route but quicker and hard to get lost on.
  • Circle of Butcherblock (Druid) or the Butcherblock translocator both land on Faydwer within a short run of Greater Faydark.
  • Succor and evacuate drop you at −644, 158, the zone line back to Greater Faydark.
