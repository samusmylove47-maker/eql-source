00What changed in v2

The Freeport ↔ Qeynos translocator exists. The patch notes state plainly that a new translocator route was added between Freeport and Qeynos, and the community race-unlock guide confirms Legends added a direct route between the two cities. Version 1 of this plan said no such route existed — that came from the wiki's Travel Guide page, which lists only the three classic boat routes and carries its own banner warning that its content is unreliable. It's stale. You were right.
The toll is stranger than "1 plat per level." Translocators are free until you reach level 20 in any class, and after that cost your highest class level in plat per trip. Not your character level — your highest. That distinction matters enormously for a reset build, and it's covered in section 02.
The routing did contain a real error. Version 1 put Blackburrow in pass 3 and justified it by geography that turns out to be wrong. It also chained three low dungeons into one pass, which is the criss-cross you spotted. Section 03 is rebuilt: one low dungeon per pass, one crossing per pass, no doubling back.

01The plate

Three passes, plotted against character level. Blocks are zones; pins on the top rail are the unlocks that change how you travel; the bar under each pass marks its single continent crossing.

02The translocator toll

This is the mechanic that should shape your travel decisions, and it interacts badly with a reset build in a way that isn't obvious.

Translocators instantly move you from one boat dock to the other end of the route. They are free before you reach level 20 in any class. After that they cost your highest class level in plat.

After pass 1

27pp

druid is your highest

During pass 2

27pp

At cap

50pp

per trip, forever

Read that third column again. The toll keys off your highest class, not your character level. Your resets drop your character level to 10, but they don't drop the toll — once the druid is 27, every crossing costs 27 platinum even while you're fighting level-12 orcs. The reset structure makes this worse, not better, and the number only climbs.

So cross while it's free. You are both level 10 with nothing above 10. Every translocator trip you take today costs nothing. That window closes permanently the first time any class of yours dings 20 — which will happen at the end of your Crushbone run.
And this is the real argument for the druid. Rituals are free. Circle of West Commons, Circle of Butcherblock, Circle of Surefall Glade and Circle of North Karana cost mana and twenty seconds, not platinum. Getting the druid to 26–27 in pass 1 isn't a convenience — it's how you opt out of a tax that scales with your highest class for the rest of the character's life.
Ten platinum is not much right now. You can't currently replace Boots of the Long Road either — those run about 14–17pp from an Adventuring Supply Merchant depending on your charisma and faction. Crushbone fixes this fast; the wiki notes orcs there carry cloth armour and weapons worth real money, and Cracked Staffs alone sell for over a platinum each. Don't skip vendor trash on your first few Crushbone sessions.

03The three passes, rebuilt

One low dungeon per pass. One continent crossing per pass. No doubling back. Your critique of v1 was correct — chaining three low zones into pass 3 while repeating Befallen and Najena in every pass was busywork dressed as a plan.

Pass 1 · levels 10–27 · Faydwer → Antonica

The Porter Run

You: Bard · Magician · Druid  ·  Her: Cleric · Magician · Wizard

10 → 20

Crushbone

Zero travel, 9:00 respawn, revamped 14 July. Trainer Hill → Wall → Throne Room. Bank the plat.

Cross at 19

Butcherblock → Freeport

Translocator at the dock. Do this before anyone hits 20 and it's free. Then East Commons → Nektulos → Lavastorm.

20 → 27

Najena

Tuned 15–30, so it covers the whole band alone. The Tenderizer at level 22 drops the Kitchen Toolbelt.

Why Najena and not Befallen here: three reasons. The Kitchen Toolbelt is a 10% haste belt usable by every class at level 22 — for a build whose damage is auto-attack and passive procs, that's the highest-multiplier item in the bracket and you want it in pass 1, not pass 3. Second, Najena's mist elementals are invulnerable to regular weapons and self-invisible; the magician-and-druid pass is the only one of your three that handles them without swearing. Third, it ends with your druid at 27.

Deliverable: Druid 26–27 (Circle of Surefall Glade and Circle of West Commons), Wizard 26 (Evacuate: North Karana), two Kitchen Toolbelts. After this, travel is free forever.

Pass 2 · levels 10–27 · Qeynos → Freeport

The Gnoll Run

You: Bard · Magician · Warrior  ·  Her: Cleric · Magician · Shaman

10 → 20

Blackburrow

Circle of Surefall Glade drops you next to the entrance. Sabertooth Overseer at 20 for the Hardened Bark Breastplate — AC 16, END 15, berserker-legal.

Cross

Qeynos → Freeport

The route you were right about. One hop, ~20pp. Or skip it entirely with Circle of West Commons.

20 → 27

Befallen

4:27 respawn, the fastest in the game at this level. Both Pristine Studded sets, plussed to +4 before you leave.

Why Blackburrow moved up: v1 buried it in pass 3 because I thought Qeynos was unreachable. It isn't. And her shaman gets Spirit of Wolf at level 9, so this is the pass where overland travel stops being painful — worth pairing with the one zone that has a genuinely long approach.

Pass 3 · levels 10–50 · no crossings

The Real One

You: Bard · Warrior · Berserker  ·  Her: Cleric · Shaman · Enchanter

10 → 25

Befallen

Covers the entire band alone. Circle of West Commons puts you at the door, you're wearing two haste belts and plussed Pristine Studded, and the 4:27 respawn means this is the fastest possible run of 10–25.

25 → 42

Splitpaw

Enter from South Karana via Circle of North Karana. Five difficulty bands, ZEM 128, seventeen levels without changing zone.

42 → 50

Lower Guk / Sol B

Then Plane of Fear and Hate trash from 46 — the XP and the planar gear come out of the same kills.

Why Najena is deliberately absent: your final trio is Bard/Warrior/Berserker and Cleric/Shaman/Enchanter. That's a lot of melee and not much reliable magic damage on your side. Najena's mist elementals cannot be hurt by regular weapons and require See Invisible to even target. Do that zone in the caster passes and skip it in the melee one. Splitpaw's gnolls, by contrast, need snare and interrupts — your bard's snare song and her shaman's root cover it exactly.

Deliverable: level 50, a free Class Unlock Token, and a pile of alternate storage.

What each pass costs you in tolls: pass 1 is free if you cross before 20. Pass 2 is one hop at roughly 20pp, or free if you'd rather use Circle of West Commons and skip the translocator. Pass 3 has no crossings at all. Total platinum spent on travel across the entire plan: about twenty.

05Travel tools, with real numbers

Version 1 described some of these vaguely. Here they are with the actual cooldowns, which changes how useful two of them are.

  • Origin — self-only gate to your home city, 18 minute cooldown. Short enough to be a genuine part of your rotation.
  • Gather Party — summons your party to you, six hour cooldown. I oversold this in v1. At six hours it's an emergency tool, not a travel plan. Save it for a corpse recovery that would otherwise cost you an hour.
  • Difficulty swap as a teleport. Changing zone difficulty teleports you to that zone's evac point, and you can do it roughly every five minutes out of combat. That's a free intra-zone recall in Blackburrow's three floors and Befallen's key-locked descent — no spell, no mana, no plat.
  • Boots of the Long Road — 14–17pp from an Adventuring Supply Merchant, price varies with charisma and faction. Fragile speed buff that breaks in combat or on ability use. You can upgrade them to move the click onto a better pair of boots later.
  • Athletics — the skill that replaced the old run-speed AAs. At high skill you move at Spirit of Wolf speed unbuffed. It levels passively; just be aware it exists so you don't over-invest in speed items.
  • Loot is per person. This is worth more to a duo than any travel tool: sharing a camp doesn't split drops, it doubles the rolls. Camp named mobs together, always.

Wizard — her side

  • Self gates 18–23: North Karana 18, Toxxulia 19, Greater Faydark 20, Stonebrunt 21, West Commons 21, Nektulos 22, North Ro 22, Cazic Temple 23, West Karana 23.
  • North Karana Portal at 25 is the only group portal in range.
  • Evacuate: North Karana at 26 — one more level for the only group escape either of you can reach in this bracket.

Melee abilities that break silently

  • Auto-skill checkboxes reset to off every time you change loadouts. Kick, Bash, Tiger Claw, Smite all stop firing. Re-toggle in Actions → Abilities after every swap, and build a macro. For a build leaning on passive damage this is the failure mode that costs the most and shows the least.
  • Cleave — Warrior, 12-second cooldown, up to three targets, free, can crit. Damage comes off your weapon's raw damage, so a slow two-hander cleaves far harder than dual wield.
  • Kick, Bash and Berserker Frenzy are all on 8-second cooldowns.

07Standing orders

  • Cross to Antonica before anyone hits 20. It's free today and it never will be again.
  • Bind at camp, every session. One cast, saves every corpse run and town trip.
  • Camp named together. Loot is per person — a duo on one named is two rolls, not a split.
  • Run D1 as a floor. D0 mobs flee at low health; D1 don't. Each tier is +20% XP.
  • Instance rather than queue. Launch week. Najena also respawns about four times faster inside one.
  • Loop, don't camp. All five revamped zones have guaranteed named with no placeholders.
  • Don't lock your primary carelessly. Level 11 locks race, deity and primary permanently. Barbarians cannot be Bards — if the race change is still on, your primary must be Warrior or Berserker.
