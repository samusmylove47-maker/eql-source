How to read these, and what they are not. A dashed outline groups named mobs whose recorded positions sit close together — it means “these are near each other”, not “this is a room”. An outline is given a name only when at least two of its members’ notes independently mention the same place; 3 groups across the ten zones cleared that bar, and the rest stay unnamed rather than guessed at. No wall, door or corridor is drawn anywhere on this page, because the project holds coordinates and not a survey of the geometry between them.
