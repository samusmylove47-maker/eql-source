01Why this zone matters

Two numbers make Befallen the most valuable low-level dungeon in the game, and neither is the level range.

The first is the 4 minute 27 second zone spawn timer. Crushbone runs 9:00 and Blackburrow runs 22:00. Befallen turns over roughly twice as fast as Crushbone and five times as fast as Blackburrow, so a named-clearing loop here pays far more per hour than the same loop anywhere else in the bracket.

The second is that it covers 7 to 25+ on its own, with the V`Zher court running to level 28 at the bottom. One zone, one bind point, an entire run from the low teens to the mid-twenties without moving camp.

The Legends revamp shows in the roster: Baron Telyx V`Zher and his footman, soldier and knight, plus Asaka L`Rei, Korven Nisere, Kahaptra Z`Taj and the Arisen Thaumaturgist are all additions. They carry the Pristine Studded Leather set and the Enchanted Fine Steel weapon line.

Befallen was not in the 28 July launch-day dungeon pass, so its placeholders and respawn maximums are untouched and the 4:27 timer is current rather than a pre-patch ceiling — unusual among the plates in this series. Two launch-week changes still reach it: loot now rolls to your difficulty tier or better with a diminishing chance at higher tiers (29 July), and chain-stunning is dead (28 July), which matters here because the fix for a fleeing necromancer was a second stun.

03The three keys

Three locked doors, three shadowknights. Every shadowknight drops its own key and every key below it in the chain, so the deeper you go the less backtracking is needed.

Door A

SK, Dark Elf male

−334, 183 · Splintered Wooden Key. West of the well, same room as Skeleton L`rodd.

Door B

SK, Human female

−552, −54 · Charred Bone Key. In the pit-trap room; opens the door across the hall.

Door C

SK, Ogre

−511, 152 · Smoked Glass Key. Opens the final door at the bottom of the ruined stair.

Shortcut

SK, Troll · SK, DE female

Troll at −372, −105 and the dark elf females on level three drop any of the three.

  • Two of the three keys are permanent. The third is not. The Splintered Wooden and Charred Bone keys carry no flags at all. The Smoked Glass Key lost its Temporary flag on 16 June — but the wiki's item record still shows it as NO RENT, and the classic prose describes it as a no-camp item that vanishes if you log out. Temporary and NO RENT are different flags and the patch only named the first. Treat a bottom-floor run as still needing to be a single sitting until someone confirms otherwise in game.unresolved
  • Every locked door searches your bags for the key, and rogues or bards with sufficient skill can click lockpicks on them instead. Both behaviours were standardised across the zone on 16 June.
  • Every key can be obtained from the dark elf female shadowknight on level three, so falling down a pit is never a dead end.
  • Two ways down without keys: the pit traps in the human SK's room, or the broken stair. The stair does significant fall damage — use the pits.
  • Key-dropping NPCs cannot be charmed (11 June), so there is no shortcut around the chain.
  • The doors are not the ones you remember. The 22 May patch made all Befallen doors properly pickable and added the note that they aren't all the doors you may remember — the door layout was changed from classic. Any classic map of this zone may be wrong about which doors exist.

06Dangers

Everything is in aggro range of everything. From the top of the entrance stairs you can usually see four skeletons, each within attack-trigger range of the others. Pull one at a level where they still give experience and you get all four. The whole dungeon is built this way.
Sitting increases your aggro radius. Sitting, meditating or looting makes mobs attack from further away, and aggressive greens will attack a seated player they would otherwise ignore. Stand before looting.
Mummies cause rabies. Lesser mummies proc a disease on their melee target. Bring Cure Disease or eat the full duration.
NPC necromancers do not play by player rules. They cast while running, spend no mana and cannot be interrupted. They also con lower than they fight, and they flee early — which starts trains. Kill them first and fast.
Trains here come back. The zone is small enough that a train's frenzy radius covers most of it. When the person being chased zones or dies, the whole train walks back down and hits everyone on the way.

07Getting there

  • A small, partly hidden tunnel along the southern wall of the West Commonlands. At night an unholy glow marks it; by day it sits behind an upward-jutting sand dune and must be found by climbing.
  • Circle of West Commons (Druid, group) lands at the doorstep. West Commons Gate (Wizard, self) is the earlier single-target option.
  • Otherwise: translocate from the Butcherblock dock to East Freeport and run west through the Commonlands.
  • Bind at camp. With a 4:27 respawn and a key chain that partially resets on logout, this zone rewards long unbroken sessions more than any other in the bracket.
  • Befallen has personal instances — confirmed by a 25 May fix to the facing direction on entry. Useful given how small the zone is and how far a train travels.
  • Succor and evacuate drop you at −75, 35, the zone line to West Commons.
