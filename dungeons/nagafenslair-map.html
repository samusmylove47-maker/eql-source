01Reading the map

Everything in this zone sees through invisibility. Greater kobolds see hide as well. You cannot sneak past anything and you cannot stealth a corpse run. Every trip is a two-way fight, so the map's real job is showing you where the exits are.
  • /loc returns Y, X, Z. +Y is north (up), +X is west (left).
  • The whole zone sits in negative Y. You enter near the top and work south and east; the dragon is at the bottom.
  • Dots are surveyed /loc. Passages are schematic.
  • Bring fire resistance. The wiki notes veterans keep whole suits of fire-resist jewellery for this zone.

02The map

Four areas, in the order you meet them. Kobolds fill the outer caves, with the three royal ledges — champion, priest, king — clustered tightly at the south of that block. Bats and bugs sit between the kobolds and the Efreeti. The Efreeti pool is west, behind the lava guardians. The Fire Giant castle occupies the entire south-east and holds everything above level 47.

03Getting in and out

StepDirections
Reach the zoneFrom Lavastorm Mountains, go to the large lava pit at the north end — confusingly also called Solusek's Eye. Two cave paths sit on its southern edge. Take the western one.
The outer cavesKobolds, and a lot of them. The wiki recommends a group for these alone — they hit hard and take a lot of damage.
The royal ledgesThree ledges hold the champion, priest and king. The priest is behind a painted false wall between the two hallways leading to the king and the champion. Targin the Rock stands left of the throne in the king's room.
Bats and bugsBetween the kobolds and the Efreeti. The death beetle sits in a small room in this stretch.
The Efreeti poolWest, ringed by three lava guardians who double as Djarn's placeholder, with Imp Protectors on the approach. There is no way to flee this area other than evacuating.
The castleSouth-east. Many doors inside are not on any map. The drawbridge room has two levers; the bridge raises itself again after a while, so do not leave it as your only exit.
Getting outSeveral zone lines to Solusek's Eye (Sol A), lettered on the wiki map. They are the standard escape — but they are used the same way from the other side, so you may arrive in someone else's emergency.
The safest hunting is nearest the Sol A lines. You can pull there and zone out if it goes wrong. The wiki's own assessment: a savvy group earns mountains of experience in that stretch in a short time.

06Caveats

  • Lord Nagafen, King Tranix, Warlord Skarlon and the Crown of King Tranix are raid-instance content. Nagafen stopped spawning in open world and personal instances on 11 June.
  • The wiki's group-size claims are classic single-class estimates. "At least four full groups" for Nagafen describes Project 1999, not Legends, where characters run three classes at once and difficulty alters mob behaviour rather than level.
  • Midghh the Dark has no coordinate. Extremely rare, no faction hits, paths from the Pool area. Not plotted.
  • Imp Protector, Greater Kobold, Solusek Kobold and lava duct crawler have no published positions — "See Discussion" or "Various" only. Their regions are shaded but they are not plotted.
  • Zordakalicus Ragefire's location is given only as "Lair." He is a triggered spawn from the Cleric epic.
  • Passages are schematic. Many castle doors are not on any map, per the wiki itself.
