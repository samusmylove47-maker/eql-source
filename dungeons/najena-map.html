01Reading the map

Najena is three maps stacked, not one. A main level and two underground areas that do not connect to each other. A and B are stairs — walk them both ways. C and D are pit falls — down only, no climbing back.

06Caveats

  • The guard captain has no published coordinate. He is placed from the wiki's text and community maps; his companions Grush and the Priestess are recorded at −385, 230 and −493, 170.
  • Sixteen elemental spawn points exist and none are published. Expect elementals anywhere.
  • Rathyl reincarnate's listed coordinate contradicts itself — trust "spawns on Rathyl's corpse", not −154, −119.
  • Room 3, the Torture Rooms, has no recorded contents. The Crystal Room's position is inferred from three magician spawns clustered together.
  • Corridors are schematic. Where this drawing and the game disagree about a hallway, the game is right.
