Your log
Turn logging on in game with /log on first. A log only knows what happened while it was running.
Recommended. /outputfile inventory at a banker covers everything you looted before logging — wind runes included. It is a snapshot of your bags, so the larger of the two counts wins and nothing is counted twice.
Wind runes
The client names all fifteen in dbstr_us.txt ids 75–89, and they match this tool's dataset exactly. They are ordinary bag items, so an /outputfile inventory dump counts them for you and you can leave this alone. Set them by hand only if you have no dump — runes drop zone-wide from any mob, so a log that began after you farmed them cannot see what you already hold.
Whose kills count
A drop rate is drops over kills you could have looted. The ledger reads your group and raid roster straight out of the log, so normally you need nothing here. Name people only if you started logging after grouping up — the client never re-lists a group you joined, so those kills would otherwise go uncounted. Kills it cannot place are excluded and reported, never quietly folded in.
