The Plane of Sky

The whole zone in the order you do it: the Key Master, the spur at 1.5, and every island through to the Eye and the Hand of Veeshan. Nine islands, six of the seven key drops confirmed against our own logs, and what each boss actually cost to kill.

9 islands43 fights measured
Measured at base difficultyOpen →
Sky is not a raid zone, whatever else you have read. We killed 15 of its bosses 43 times with a median of four attackers and a thinnest fight of two. The most expensive boss in the zone costs about a 15th of Cazic-Thule at Refined.
Everything here is base difficulty. D0–D4 does not raise mob levels — it makes mobs run player-style class kits, widens aggro ranges and pre-upgrades loot. Named mobs are often multiclass from D2 and raid bosses start appearing triple-class at D3. We have never played Sky above D0, so nothing on that page describes Awakened or above, and it says so where the figures are.
