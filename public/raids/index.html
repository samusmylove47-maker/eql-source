An encounter is published because someone killed it, not because a description exists elsewhere. Sky is not a raid zone, whatever else you have read — its dearest boss costs about a 10th of what Cazic-Thule costs at Refined, and every boss on the ring is measured below that.

Difficulty does not raise mob levels. D0–D4 makes mobs run player-style class kits, widens aggro ranges and pre-upgrades loot. Named mobs are often multiclass from D2, and raid bosses start appearing triple-class at D3. Nothing measured here reaches above D0.
