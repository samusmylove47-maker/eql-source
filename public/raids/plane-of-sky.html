The Plane of Sky

9

Islands on a one-way loop, each gated by a key the island below it drops

Every other reference calls Sky a raid zone. Its dearest boss costs about a 10th of what Cazic-Thule costs at Refined. Nothing here sees through invisibility, so you can walk past almost everything and kill only what you came for — and falling is what kills people, on gusts of wind you cannot see. The zone, drawn

The ring

Buy the first three keys from the Key Master on island 1. After that each boss drops the key to the next island, so the order is fixed.

Every confirmed key dropped from exactly the boss the chain names TIER M. The one gap is island 1: the Thunder Spirit Princess has never been measured.

Five hazards

Cautions

  • Falling The gusts of wind on island 3 are invisible and need see-invis to spot. They are why people fall off it.
  • Social radius Azaracks on 2 and spirocs on 5 bring the island with them. Work the rim; the middle is where a pull becomes everything.
  • Adds that do not stop Island 4 keeps producing them. Kill to a timer rather than clearing to zero.
  • The spur’s approach A blade storm guards the way on to island 1.5.
  • Poison on island 6 The bees cast Deadly Poison, resisted more often than any other spell in the zone.

The one procedure

Island 8, in full

The only part of the zone where the order matters.

  1. Go invisible before you take the portal up. You arrive unengaged and choose your own opening.
  2. Walk to the back of the island, to the teleporter that returns you to the landing. The Hand of Veeshan wanders there.
  3. Kill the Hand first.
  4. Then the Eye, where it stands.
  5. Slow them both. That is the whole fight.

The difference

Damage to kill is not hit points. It counts every attacker and sits above a boss’s health rather than measuring it, so it is a ceiling on the zone rather than a target for one character. A figure marked or more comes from a fight joined after it started, so it is a floor.

From the mesh

How high it actually is

Read from the zone’s own mesh, west to east. 21 separate bodies of walkable floor across 2,878 units of height, from -1,031 to 1,847. Each mark is sized by how much floor it holds, and a tower counts separately from the ground it stands on. For comparison, the deepest dungeon surveyed on this site spans about 950 units top to bottom.

The two axes are not at the same scale. Height is drawn at 0.38 of its true size against the horizontal, so Sky reads about 2.6 times flatter here than it is.
The marks are not labelled, and that is deliberate. The mesh says where every piece of floor is; it does not say which piece is “island 4”. That lives in the teleporter network, and one /loc per island — 9 readings — would label this chart permanently.
