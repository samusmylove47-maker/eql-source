Sky is a ring,
not a ladder.

Eight islands on a one-way teleporter loop, with a ninth hanging off it. Island 8 returns you to island 1, which is why the six-boss circuit everyone runs is not a list of instructions — it is one and a half laps.

8 islands on the loop3 keys bought, 6 dropped 2,878 units of height

The ring

Every teleporter runs one way. Ringed islands are the six the standard circuit kills.

The key chain

Three keys are bought from the Key Master on island 1. After that every boss drops the key to the island above it, so the order is fixed even where the tactics are not.

  • 1Thunder Spirit Princess Key of Swords Where you arrive, and where the Key Master sells the first three keys.
  • 1.5Noble Dojorn — Off island 1, and it returns you to island 2. A blade storm guards it.
  • 2Protector of Sky Key of Misfortune Azaracks. Large aggro radius and a large social radius, so a pull tends to become the island.
  • 3The Gorgalosk Key of Beasts Gorgons, gazers and a heart harpie in the tower. Gusts of wind are invisible and need see-invis to spot.
  • 4Keeper of Souls Avian Key Pegasi, and adds that keep coming. The Overseer of Air stands at the windmill tower and is one of the six on the circuit.
  • 5The Spiroc Lord Key of the Swarm Spirocs have a low aggro radius and a high social one, so the outer edge is quiet and the middle is not.
  • 6Bazzt Zzzt Key of Scale Three bees. The middle one is the boss and dies into three successive forms; the last of them drops the key.
  • 7Sister of the Spire Veeshan's Key Nothing here aggros except the boss.
  • 8Eye of Veeshan — The Hand of Veeshan wanders this island and is the last of the six.
Nothing in the Plane of Sky sees through invisibility. That is the opposite of Fear and Hate, where see-invis is the main hazard, and it is why the circuit can skip most of the zone rather than clear it.

The six-boss circuit

The islands worth killing on a repeat run, in the order the ring makes cheapest.

6→7→8→1→1.5→4→8

Bee island first, because the bee is the best thing to take with you; then up through 7 and 8; then the loop returns you to 1 for the spur at 1.5; then round again for the Overseer of Air on 4 and the Hand of Veeshan on 8. Two visits to island 8, and they are different kills — the Eye on the first pass, the Hand on the second.

We do not publish a clear time. The one figure we have was given by someone who said in the same sentence that they had not timed it, and a time is a fact about a player rather than about the zone.

How high it actually is

Read from the zone’s own mesh on 11 August 2026. Nobody has published this before, and it is ours rather than anyone’s drawing.

21 separate bodies of walkable floor across 2,878 units of height, from -1,031 to 1,847. Seen from the side, west to east. Each mark is sized by how much floor it holds. For comparison, the deepest dungeon we have measured spans about 600 units top to bottom.

These are not labelled, and that is deliberate. The mesh says exactly where every piece of floor is. It does not say which piece is “island 4” — that lives in the teleporter network, and no /loc reading from Sky exists to anchor it. Ten /loc readings, one per island, would label this chart permanently. Drawing a guess would be worth less than drawing nothing.

A body of floor is not always an island, either. A tower counts separately from the ground it stands on, which is why there are 21 marks and nine islands.

What we do not know

  • 1Which measured body is which island 10 /loc readings
  • 2What the tenth island is one sighting One account counts ten, listing 1–8, 1.5 and an Efreeti island. We can place the Overseer of Air at the windmill tower on 4, but whether the Efreeti island is a separate place is unresolved.
  • 3Boss hit points at any difficulty a raid log Every published stat block for these bosses traces to wiki pages created before the game existed.
  • 4Which Sky drops are quest turn-ins a turn-in list One account lists items said to be safe to sell. We are not republishing it: a reader who vendors a quest component on our word has been badly served.
