The Plane
of Sky.

Nine islands on a one-way teleporter loop, each gated by a key the island below it drops. The whole zone in the order you do it, with what every boss cost us to kill. It is not a raid zone, whatever else you have read.

90 fights, 15 bosses median 3 attackers all at base difficulty

How the zone works

You arrive on island 1 and the Key Master is standing there. Buy the first three keys from him. After that every island’s boss drops the key to the next one, so the order is fixed: 1 → the spur at 1.5 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8. Island 1.5 hangs off the landing and its teleporter puts you on 2 rather than back on 1, so it costs nothing to take on the way past. Six of the seven predicted key drops landed in our logs, each from exactly the boss named below; the seventh is unchecked because we have never found the Thunder Spirit Princess. TIER M

Nothing in the Plane of Sky sees through invisibility. That is the opposite of Fear and Hate, and it is the single most useful fact about the zone: you can walk past almost everything and kill only what you came for.

Island 8, in full

Two bosses on one island, and the only part of the zone where the order matters. Reported by the site’s owner, who has cleared it about ten times. TIER C

  1. Go invisible before you take the portal up. Nothing here sees invis, so you arrive unengaged and choose your own opening.
  2. Walk around to the back of the island, to the teleporter that returns you to the landing. The Hand of Veeshan wanders there.
  3. Kill the Hand of Veeshan at the back. It drops the efreeti line.
  4. Walk back to the front and kill the Eye of Veeshan where it stands.
  5. Slow them both. That is the whole fight.

The difference

The hardest thing in Sky dies to whoever happens to be standing there.

Damage to kill is not hit points. It counts every attacker and sits above a boss’s health rather than measuring it. A figure marked or more is a fight we joined late, so it is a floor.
All of it is base difficulty. D0 is the only tier we have played Sky at, so nothing here describes Awakened or above.
None of it was one trio. Every Sky fight in our logs is a public pick-up raid of 1 to 7 players. The site’s owner reports the zone can be soloed TIER C; our thinnest logged fight is 1, so that stands unconfirmed.

How high it actually is

Read from the zone’s own mesh on 11 August 2026. Nobody has published this before, and it is ours rather than anyone’s drawing.

21 separate bodies of walkable floor across 2,878 units of height, from -1,031 to 1,847. Seen from the side, west to east. Each mark is sized by how much floor it holds. For comparison, the deepest dungeon we have measured spans about 600 units top to bottom.

The two axes are not at the same scale. Height is drawn at 0.38 of its true size against the horizontal, so Sky reads about 2.6 times flatter here than it is. That is normal for a section drawing and it is why surveyors print the figure on the drawing rather than in a caption.
These are not labelled, and that is deliberate. The mesh says where every piece of floor is; it does not say which piece is “island 4”. That lives in the teleporter network, and one /loc per island — 9 readings — would label this chart permanently and let us draw each island properly. Drawing a guess would be worth less than drawing nothing.

A body of floor is not always an island: a tower counts separately from the ground it stands on, which is why there are 21 marks and 9 places to stand.
