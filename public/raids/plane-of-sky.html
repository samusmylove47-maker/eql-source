Sky is not a
raid zone.

Every reference in this community described a zone that needs a full raid, and so did this page until today. Over two nights we recorded 43 fights against 15 of its bosses. The median fight had 4 attackers, the thinnest had 2, and the most expensive boss in the zone cost 26,158 damage.

Damage to kill is not hit points. It is the damage that had to be dealt, which is what you actually plan around, and it sits above the boss’s health rather than measuring it. Where a row reads or more we joined after the boss was engaged and the figure is a floor. Where it reads once we have a single fight and no way to tell a cheap boss from a kill we arrived at the end of — Bzizzzt’s only record is 614 damage over three seconds, and its siblings take twenty thousand.
Every one of these fights is at base difficulty. D0 is the only tier we have played Sky at, so nothing on this page describes Awakened or above. Difficulty raises how much of a boss’s class kit appears, and on this evidence we cannot say what that does here.
None of these were our trio alone. Every Sky fight in the logs is a public pick-up raid of 2 to 7 players. The site’s owner reports the zone can be soloed TIER C; our thinnest logged fight is 2 attackers, so that stands unconfirmed until a one-attacker kill appears in a log.

The ring

Every teleporter runs one way. Ringed islands are the ones the standard circuit kills; a dashed ring is a boss we have never found.

The key chain, confirmed

Three keys are bought from the Key Master on island 1. After that every boss drops the key to the island above it. The chain was inherited from two post-launch accounts and had never been checked against a log.

  • 1Thunder Spirit Princess Key of Swords Where you arrive, and where the Key Master sells the first three keys. not seen
  • 1.5Noble Dojorn — Off island 1, and it returns you to island 2. A blade storm guards it.
  • 2Protector of Sky Key of Misfortune Azaracks. Large aggro radius and a large social radius, so a pull tends to become the island. confirmed ×2 M
  • 3Gorgalosk Key of Beasts Gorgons, gazers and a heart harpie in the tower. Gusts of wind are invisible and need see-invis to spot. confirmed ×2 M
  • 4Keeper of Souls Avian Key Pegasi, and adds that keep coming. The Overseer of Air stands at the windmill tower and is a separate kill on the same island. confirmed ×2 M
  • 5The Spiroc Lord Key of the Swarm Spirocs have a low aggro radius and a high social one, so the outer edge is quiet and the middle is not. The Spiroc Guardian is here too. confirmed ×2 M
  • 6Bazzt Zzzt Key of Scale Bees, and more of them than any source we hold lists — six named variants went down here. confirmed ×3 M
  • 7Sister of the Spire Veeshan's Key Nothing here aggros except the boss. confirmed ×3 M
  • 8Eye of Veeshan — The Hand of Veeshan wanders this island and is the last of the circuit.

Six of the seven predicted drops landed, each from exactly the boss the chain names. The seventh is unchecked rather than wrong: we never found the Thunder Spirit Princess, so the Key of Swords has no measurement behind it either way.

Nothing in the Plane of Sky sees through invisibility. That is the opposite of Fear and Hate, where see-invis is the main hazard, and it is why the circuit can skip most of the zone rather than clear it.

The circuit, and the bee island

The islands worth killing on a repeat run, in the order the ring makes cheapest.

6→7→8→1→1.5→4→8

Bee island first, then up through 7 and 8; the loop returns you to 1 for the spur at 1.5; then round again for the Overseer of Air on 4 and the Hand of Veeshan on 8. Two visits to island 8, and they are different kills — the Eye on the first pass, the Hand on the second.

Island 6 runs six named bees, not one. No source we hold mentions this, and they are not one mob in successive forms as this page said until today: the Key of Scale came off Bazzt Zzzt itself, three times.

Do not bring poison to the bees. They cast Deadly Poison at us constantly and we resisted it 164 times across four of the six variants M. That is the largest resist count in the zone by a wide margin, and it is the one piece of the bee island that changes what you pack.
We do not publish a clear time. The one figure we have was given by someone who said in the same sentence that they had not timed it, and a time is a fact about a player rather than about the zone.

How high it actually is

Read from the zone’s own mesh on 11 August 2026. Nobody has published this before, and it is ours rather than anyone’s drawing.

21 separate bodies of walkable floor across 2,878 units of height, from -1,031 to 1,847. Seen from the side, west to east. Each mark is sized by how much floor it holds. For comparison, the deepest dungeon we have measured spans about 600 units top to bottom.

The two axes are not at the same scale. Height is drawn at 0.38 of its true size against the horizontal, so Sky reads about 2.6 times flatter here than it is. That is normal for a section drawing and it is why surveyors print the figure on the drawing rather than in a caption. It had been missing from this chart since 11 August 2026, which made the proportion an invented one.
These are not labelled, and that is deliberate. The mesh says exactly where every piece of floor is. It does not say which piece is “island 4” — that lives in the teleporter network, and no /loc reading from Sky exists to anchor it. One /loc per island — 9 of them, or 10 if the Efreeti island is a separate place — would label this chart permanently. Drawing a guess would be worth less than drawing nothing.

A body of floor is not always an island: a tower counts separately from the ground it stands on, which is why there are 21 marks and 9 places to stand.

What we do not know

  • 1Anything about Sky above D0 one logged run All 43 fights are at base difficulty. One session at Awakened would say whether the tiers change this zone the way they change a dungeon.
  • 2The Thunder Spirit Princess one kill Island 1’s boss, and the only one on the ring that has never appeared in a log of ours — not killed, not seen, not named. Its key is the one link in the chain still unconfirmed.
  • 3Which measured body is which island 9 /loc readings
  • 4Whether there is a tenth island one sighting One account counts ten, listing 1–8, 1.5 and an Efreeti island. The efreeti gear does not settle it: we watched it drop on three islands that are already on the ring, which is evidence about where the gear comes from and none at all about whether a tenth place exists.
  • 5Boss hit points at any difficulty nobody has these Damage to kill bounds them from above, and every published stat block for these bosses traces to wiki pages created before the game existed.
  • 6Which Sky drops are quest turn-ins a turn-in list One account lists items said to be safe to sell. We are not republishing it: a reader who vendors a quest component on our word has been badly served.
