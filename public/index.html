50 Upgrades

Pick a trio and a race, fill twenty-three slots, and compare what each candidate does to the character rather than to the item beside it. Every item upgrades from +0 to +10, and the stat sheet recomputes as you touch it.

It holds 3,663 items, 1,713 of them carrying stat values. Eligibility is the union of your three classes, so a paladin in the mix opens plate for everyone, and points past a cap score nothing.

Your sets live in this browser and travel as a link. Every item window names where its numbers came from, and the full accounting of what is sourced and what is not is one click away.

Plane of Sky · reads your own log

Sky Ledger

It follows your combat log while you play and says which of the 95 Plane of Sky class-unlock tests you can hand in now — and what the missing pieces drop from. In a browser with nothing to install, or as an overlay on the game.

It knows a turn-in piece can only be spent once. 29 of its 128 turn-in items are wanted by more than one test. Holding one does not make several quests ready, and every other tracker — including the one this replaces, which was ours — counts it against all of them. It also refuses to print a drop rate it cannot measure: a dry streak reads as a bound, <28% · 0/9, never 0%.

EQLS Auras

It reads your combat log and draws your buffs over the game as icons that count down, so you can see what is about to drop off without opening a window.

It reads files the client already has: the log it writes as you play, your spellbook, and the game's own spell icons. It does not read or alter the game's memory, inject code into it, or send it input. It fetches its typeface from Google each time it launches, which discloses your IP address to Google; beyond that it sends nothing — no telemetry, no analytics, no update check. That fetch is the main window only: the overlay drawn over the game requests nothing at all.

The idea is WeakAuras'. The code is not: a from-scratch implementation for EverQuest Legends, sharing no code and no trigger format with it, and neither affiliated with nor endorsed by its authors.
