Where this
started.

Ten hand-built coordinate plots. Every named mob’s /loc read off the wiki, transformed into page space and drawn by hand, with a numbered legend beside it. They were the first thing this project made, and the pages were called plates because that is what they were.

10 platesRetired 10 Aug 2026 Kept verbatim

Why they were retired, and why they are still here. geometry.py learned to read the game’s own mesh archives, and the floor plans that came out are better in every direction that matters: the walls are the game’s walls rather than an outline drawn around some dots, the storeys separate, the named filter by storey, and every coordinate is checked against walkable floor at build time. Six impossible Najena positions were caught that way and withheld. The hand plots could not have caught them, because they had nothing to check against.

These are kept because they are the record of how the survey was done before it could be done properly — and because anyone who wants to judge whether we improved or merely changed should be able to put the two side by side. They are stored exactly as they last shipped. Nothing here has been re-rendered or tidied.

The Undead Tower looks crowded because it is vertical. Ten of the twenty plotted positions fall inside a box roughly 170 game units square in the north-west. That is not a plotting artefact — the tower is a stack of tightly packed rooms, so its named separate on Z, which a plan view cannot show. Read that cluster as one column, not one floor. The same is true in miniature at the bottom centre, where High Scale Kirn, the mimic, Kejar and Schnozz sit within about 40 units of each other.
Most named here are not on the plate at all, and that is deliberate. The elemental line — Bejeweled elemental, Rocksoul, Stonegrinder Minion, Gibartik, Muck covered elemental, Stonesoul the Unmoving, The Stone Caller — can spawn at the location of any elemental mob, and more than one copy of the same named can be up at once. The undead line behaves the same way off the revenant and wanderer pools. A named undead cannot take an elemental point or the reverse, but within its own pool it is everywhere. Plotting those would be inventing coordinates, so they live in the roster instead.
