People who
told us things.

Almost everything on this site that is genuinely about EverQuest Legends, rather than inherited from a wiki that predates it, came from a player who went and looked. Several of these findings corrected pages we had already published.

  • THE GAME ISTheir work → The mote and upgrade guide — what a mote is worth to a spell against an item, and why converting them upward costs you.
  • CavepigTheir work → Keying and speed-clearing the Plane of Sky. The Key Master, the Overseer of Air, and the island count our own page was short of.
  • Skyfox The planar loot guide that showed the class-group split was gone, and that haunted chests and phoboplasms drop the two shared sets.
  • Annalise Blunt and slashing weapons underwater in Kedge Keep. The Fiery Avenger completed as a non-paladin. Raid lockouts running per difficulty.
  • BarakDur The idea behind the planar gear tool — comparing all three of a trio’s class sets at once instead of one. The tool was withdrawn in favour of 50 Upgrades, which does the same thing across every slot; the idea carried forward into it.
  • Classic XPTheir work → Farming Lustrous Russet in the Plane of Hate, and the rooftop route across the city that sent us looking for a rooftop layer in the mesh.
  • BrutallStaticTheir work → Every voidling location, and the confirmation that raid bosses live only in raid instances.
  • Avenrae and Shara The combat logs behind every measured figure on this site.

If you found something here that is wrong, or something the wiki has wrong, that is the most useful thing anyone can send us. Send a finding and you will end up on this page. Please do not attach a combat log to a public issue — they can carry private chat. Say you have one and we will ask.
