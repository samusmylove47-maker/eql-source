A ruined keep under the West Commonlands, levels 7 to 25 with named to 28. Come for the Pristine Studded Leather set, six pieces out of one zone, on the shortest respawn we have recorded. Everything is in aggro range of everything: pull one skeleton and you get four. Three locked doors gate the descent.

Where you are

One well, three ways down, and a bottom floor patrolled from the south.

A tunnel in the southern wall of the West Commonlands
The entrance. An unholy glow marks it at night; by day it hides behind a sand dune and must be climbed to. Circle of West Commons lands at the doorstep; West Commons Gate is the earlier single-target port. With no druid or wizard, translocate from the Butcherblock dock to East Freeport and run west through the Commonlands
Four skeletons seen from the entrance stairs
The first room. Skeleton L`rodd is in its alcove; the well beside it drops to Priest Amiaz, first right
A room floored with pit traps
The human shadowknight's room, and the way down without keys. The broken stair also descends, and does significant fall damage
A ruined stair ending at a locked door
The bottom floor. Teir`Dal patrols hold its southern half

Befallen runs personal instances, confirmed by a 25 May fix to the facing on entry. Bind at camp: the key chain partly resets on logout.

Five hazards

Cautions

  • Everything is in aggro range of everything Four skeletons are usually visible from the entrance stairs, each inside the others' trigger radius. Pull one and you get four. The whole dungeon is built this way.
  • Sitting widens your aggro radius Sitting, meditating and looting all do it, and aggressive greens will take a seated player they would otherwise ignore. Stand before looting.
  • NPC necromancers do not play by player rules They cast while running, spend no mana and cannot be interrupted. They con lower than they fight and flee early, which starts trains. Kill them first.
  • Trains here come back A train's frenzy radius covers most of a zone this small, so when the player being chased zones or dies the whole train walks back down.
  • Mummies cause rabies Lesser mummies proc a disease on their melee target. Bring Cure Disease or eat the full duration.
