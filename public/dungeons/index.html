What verified means

A zone counts as verified only when all three gates pass: its wiki page was fetched in full and its roster re-compared, its edit history was fetched — not merely the footer date — and every coordinate lands on drawn floor, within 120 units of geometry extracted from the game’s own mesh files.

By that standard 10 of 13 are verified, 0 are partial and 3 are not verified at all. Partial surveys are complete and useful; they have simply not cleared every gate. Which gate is open is recorded per zone rather than averaged into a single number that would read better than the truth.

Open gates

  • 11 Plane of Fear Gate 3 cannot be attempted here: no named coordinate is recorded for this zone, so there is nothing to check against the floor. Gates 1 and 2 are done - every boss page was checked against the MediaWiki revision API on 10 Aug 2026 and every one is a Project 1999 import. Measured in play: Cazic-Thule at Adaptive, Fused and Refined, with his full spell kit. unstarted
  • 12 Plane of Hate Gate 3 cannot be attempted here: no named coordinate is recorded, so there is nothing to check against the floor. Gates 1 and 2 are done - the boss pages were checked against the revision API on 10 Aug 2026 and are Project 1999 imports. The zone has a second archive, hateplaneb, whose relationship to this one is not established. Measured in play: Innoruuk at Fused and Refined and ten of his court, with which boss drops which planar set. unstarted
  • 13 Kedge Keep The floor plan is read from the game's own mesh and Phinigel's position is checked against it. Phinigel Autropos is measured now, killed in a group instance at the top tier, but the zone around him is not: the open-world run is short, no upgradeable drops are recorded and the trash is barely sampled. The low grade here is accurate, not an artefact. unstarted
