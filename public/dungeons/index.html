What verified means

A zone counts as verified only when all three gates pass: its wiki page was fetched in full and its roster re-compared, its edit history was fetched — not merely the footer date — and every coordinate lands on drawn floor, within 120 units of geometry extracted from the game’s own mesh files.

By that standard 10 of 13 are verified, 0 are partial and 3 are not verified at all. Partial surveys are complete and useful; they have simply not cleared every gate. Which gate is open is recorded per zone rather than averaged into a single number that would flatter us.

Open gates

  • 11 Plane of Fear Nothing here is ours yet. The floor plan is read from the game's own mesh, but no coordinate on this page has been checked against it, no named position is recorded, and no figure is measured. Gates 1 to 3 are all unstarted. unstarted
