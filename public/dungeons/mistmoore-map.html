01Reading the map

Two halves and an upper floor. The outer grounds sit west — graveyard, tomb, pit, canyon, jail, courtyard, fountain and tower. The castle sits east. Six named live on the castle's Level 2, marked with a hollow ring; that is the only vertical information any source gives for this zone.
  • /loc returns Y, X, Z. +Y is north (up). +X is west (left).
  • You zone in at −295, 123, bottom centre. The castle is east of you; the grounds are west.
  • The tomb holds a one-way secret entrance to the jail. You can go down it. You cannot come back.
  • Dots are surveyed /loc values. Room outlines and passages are schematic.

02The map

The red band is the train funnel and it is the single most useful thing on this map. Trains start deep in the castle, collect every creature on the way out, and all of them cross the middle of the outer area between the tower and the lake heading for the narrow corridor to the zone line. Standing anywhere in that band when one starts is fatal. The only shelter is behind the lake or behind the tower — or zoning.

03The day/night cycle

The castle repopulates twice a day in game time — 06:00 and 20:00. At dawn the undead give way to dark elf nobles and musicians; at dusk vampires and werewolves rise and replace many of the daytime creatures. Anything still alive respawns anyway, and anything killed repops.
TimeWho is upWhat hides you
06:00 → 20:00Dark elves, nobles, gypsies, musicians, gargoylesOrdinary invisibility

  • Carry both, and know which is live. The wrong one hides you from nothing.
  • Be standing at 06:00 and 20:00. Sitting gives level-1-equivalent aggro, so a repop tick while seated can hand you the entire room.
  • A camp cleared at 19:55 is full again at 20:00, with a different roster. Plan the hour, not the pull.
  • The trap on the jail's secret door strips invisibility outright, whichever one you are running.

05Caveats

  • Mayong Mistmoore has no level, class or coordinate on record — only a loot entry for the Fanged Skull Stiletto. His coffin room is number 15; he is not plotted.
  • The Dark Huntress has no coordinate either. She is recorded as spawning at random locations, buffing herself and hunting.
  • A werewolf's recorded coordinates belong to West Karana, West Commonlands and North Karana, not this zone. Werewolves do appear here after dusk; their castle positions are simply unrecorded.
  • Four population entries have no position: a ghastish ancille, a ghoulish ancille, a glyphed sentry (also no class) and a leering gargoyle.
  • Rooms 3, 9, 12, 14, 15, 16 and 22 — the Pit, Piano, Corner, Torture Chamber, Coffin Room, Kitchen and Bedroom — have no recorded contents beyond their names.
  • Corridors are schematic. Where this drawing and the game disagree about a passage, the game is right.
