How to read a position off it. /loc returns Y, X, Z. +Y is north and runs up the page; +X is west and runs left. The edge figures are the game’s own axes, so −117, 86 sits 117 south and 86 west of the origin. Hover a mark for its name and reading.

02The three storeys

The mesh separates into three bands of floor. The composite draws all three at once — deepest heaviest, highest dashed. Apart, and sharing one frame, they are legible.

These are measured bands of height, not the castle’s own floors, and the two are not matched here. Six named carry the survey’s label “Level 2”, which is the survey’s word and not a reading. Only two named carry a Z value at all, and both land in the lowest band; they are the only marks on the sheets. Which measured band is the castle’s Level 2 is unrecorded, and one /loc taken beside any Level 2 named would settle it.

03The day/night cycle

The castle repopulates twice a day in game time — 06:00 and 20:00. At dawn the undead give way to dark elf nobles and musicians; at dusk vampires and werewolves rise and replace many of the daytime creatures. Anything still alive respawns anyway, and anything killed repops.
TimeWho is upWhat hides you
06:00 → 20:00Dark elves, nobles, gypsies, musicians, gargoylesOrdinary invisibility
20:00 → 06:00Vampires and werewolves replace much of the day populationInvisibility versus undead
  • Carry both, and know which is live. The wrong one hides you from nothing.
  • Be standing at 06:00 and 20:00. Sitting gives level-1-equivalent aggro, so a repop tick while seated can hand you the entire room.
  • A camp cleared at 19:55 is full again at 20:00, with a different roster. Plan the hour, not the pull.
  • The trap on the jail’s secret door strips invisibility outright, whichever one you are running.

05What this chart does not show

A drawing is an assertion, so four things came off this page on 17 August 2026: hand-drawn room blocks, a train funnel band, a one-way tomb-to-jail passage, and 48 unsourced population dots. The train and the passage are real, and are described in words above. Where they run is not recorded, so they are no longer drawn.
  • Rooms, corridors and walls are absent. The mesh gives walkable floor, not room boundaries. Where the drawing and the game disagree about a passage, the game is right.
  • Three named have no position on record and are listed below rather than placed somewhere plausible.
  • A werewolf’s recorded coordinates belong to West Karana, West Commonlands and North Karana, not this zone. Werewolves do appear here after dusk; their castle positions are simply unrecorded.
  • Four population entries have no position: a ghastish ancille, a ghoulish ancille, a glyphed sentry and a leering gargoyle.
  • Rooms 3, 9, 12, 14, 15, 16 and 22 — the Pit, Piano, Corner, Torture Chamber, Coffin Room, Kitchen and Bedroom — have no recorded contents beyond their names.
