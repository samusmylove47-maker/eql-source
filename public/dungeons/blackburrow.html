01Revamp status

Revamped 2 June 2026 — the first of the dungeon passes. The patch note is short: zone population adjusted for a smoother curve from 5 to 20, familiar loot upgraded for the Legends era, and some spicy new loot added.

"Some new loot" undersells it. The revamp added an entire cast of Splitpaw refugee gnolls — a Sentry, a Sharpshooter, an Explorer, two Commanders, a Shaman and a Monk — plus the Sabertooth Overseer, Master Brewer, Mannan of the Sabertooth, Socho Darkpaw and Lord Elgnub. Between them they carry Hardened Bark Breastplate, Vagrant's Tunic, Spiked Buckler, Keg Mallet, Pub Pants, Refugee Shroud, Burrowing Goggles and the Runed Sabertooth Girdle, none of which existed before.

Community reports put the practical curve at 7–8 at the entrance, 10–14 through the middle, 15–19 among the elites deeper in, and Lord Elgnub at 22 as the cap fight — which matches the wiki's named levels closely.

Confidence note. Wiki page last edited 4 July 2026, a month after the revamp. Blackburrow was not named in the 28 July launch-day dungeon pass, so placeholders and respawn timers here are unchanged. One naming discrepancy: some community guides call the level-22 cap fight "Lord Bongle"; the wiki calls him Lord Elgnub. Same gnoll.

03The three floors

Floor One · levels 7–12

The Ring

Two entrances feeding a ring with a deep hole in the middle. The plague rat storeroom, the Sentry Lookout, and Tranixx Darkpaw in his spawn room — he stopped roaming the zone lines on 14 July.

The Hollowed Tree has a false floor that drops to floor two. Useful once, unpleasant by accident.

Floor Two · levels 12–17

Caves & Commanders

Snake Ledge with a ladder down, the Floor Spike Room, the Elite Gnoll Room, Refugee's Nook, the High Shaman room, two commander posts, and Socho's hide under a land bridge.

The densest floor, and where clean pulls stop being possible.

Floor Three · levels 17–22

Brewery & The Lord

The Bridge Room is the classic pull spot. Beyond it: the Overseer's post, the Brewer's barrel supply, Sharpshooter Ledge, the Banner Room and the Explorer's alcove.

Lord Elgnub sits northeast of the underground river landing, at the far end.

Gnoll fangs are free levels. Every gnoll in the zone drops them commonly. They turn in at South Qeynos for the Moonstones quest and at Halas for the Gnoll Bounty — experience, faction and rewards at low levels. Loot every fang.

06Dangers

Five gnolls in aggro range of each other is normal. Worse, spawn groups mix levels — an elite guard sitting inside a cluster of low gnolls makes a clean pull close to impossible. Con the whole group, not the one you are aiming at.
Blackburrow gnolls are exceptionally social. They come to each other's aid eagerly, patrollers appear from blind corners, and they will aggro through closed doors. Be very careful with AoE and fear effects — one stray spell or one runner starts a zone-wide train.
There is only one tunnel to the surface, and low-level players hunt along it. When a deep group misses a pull, everything they collected comes up that single corridor.
The architecture itself is a hazard. Bridges, narrow ledges, water and a deep central pit. NPC pathing is decent but players fall off things and wedge into cracks. Mind your step on floor three.

07Getting there

  • Qeynos Hills entrance — northeast corner, east of Surefall Glade and north of the Western Karana zone lines. No path leads to it; look for a cave mouth shaped like a gnoll's head.
  • Everfrost entrance — southeast segment of the canyons. Harder to find, guarded by a couple of gnolls with two more down the tunnel. Torches light the way now.
  • Circle of Surefall Glade (Druid, group) is the best port for this zone — Surefall Glade opens onto Qeynos Hills and the cave mouth sits east of it. Circle of North Karana or North Karana Portal work one level earlier but leave a longer run.
  • Freeport ↔ Qeynos translocator. Legends added a direct route between the two cities. Translocators are free until you reach level 20 in any class, and then cost your highest class level in plat per trip.
  • Succor and evacuate drop you at −159, 39, the zone line to Qeynos Hills.
