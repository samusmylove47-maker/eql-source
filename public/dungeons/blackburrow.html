Blackburrow

05

Three floors, one exit tunnel, and the best chest piece in the bracket

A gnoll warren between Qeynos Hills and Everfrost, levels 5 to 20 with Lord Elgnub at 22. Come for the Hardened Bark Breastplate, the focus items and the fang turn-ins. Respawn is 22:00, so a camp you lose is a camp you wait on. The gnolls are exceptionally social, and one tunnel serves the whole zone.

Key to the plan

Where you are

Two entrances, a ring on top, and two floors of cave under it.

A cave mouth shaped like a gnoll's head
The Qeynos Hills entrance, northeast corner, east of Surefall Glade and north of the Western Karana line. No path leads to it. Circle of Surefall Glade lands closest; the two North Karana ports work a level earlier and leave a longer run
A torch-lit tunnel in the canyons
The Everfrost entrance, southeast segment. Harder to find, guarded by two gnolls with two more down the tunnel. Torches light the way now
A ring around a deep hole
Floor one, 7 to 12: the plague rat storeroom, the Sentry's ledge over The Pit, Tranixx Darkpaw's spawn room
A hollowed tree
A false floor that drops to floor two. Useful once, unpleasant by accident
A snake ledge with a ladder down
Floor two, 12 to 17: Floor Spike Room, Elite Gnoll Room, Refugee's Nook, the High Shaman room, two commander posts, Socho's hide under a land bridge. The densest floor, and where clean pulls stop
The Bridge Room
The pull spot on floor three, 17 to 22. Past it: the Overseer's post, the Brewer's barrels, Sharpshooter Ledge, the Banner Room, the Explorer's alcove. Lord Elgnub is at the far end

The Freeport–Qeynos translocator is free until you reach level 20 in any class, then costs your highest class level in platinum per trip.

Four hazards

Cautions

  • Con the group, not the target Five gnolls in aggro range of each other is normal, and spawn groups mix levels — an elite guard inside a cluster of low gnolls makes a clean pull close to impossible.
  • Exceptionally social They aid each other eagerly, patrollers appear from blind corners, and they aggro through closed doors. One stray area spell or one runner starts a zone-wide train.
  • One tunnel to the surface Low-level players hunt along it, and everything a deep group fails to hold comes up that corridor.
  • The architecture Bridges, narrow ledges, water and a deep central pit. Pathing is decent; players are what fall off things.
