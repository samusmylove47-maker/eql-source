01Why you come here

  • Levels 4 to 25 in one zone. A whole early career without moving, at ZEM 128.
  • It counts as outdoors. Unusual for a dungeon, and it changes what works here.
  • The Cold Iron set, off King Gragnar. Chest, helm, gauntlets and vambraces, AC 17 down to AC 8.
  • The entrance is the whole problem. The kobold density that makes it good hunting is also the worst train risk in the zone. Crowd control or a real group, not optional.

Three quests start in this zone. Catman Alliance from Aderius Rhenar at level 1, which pays coin and faction and links to Erudin; Erudite Prisoners from a prisoner at level 10; and Miranda's Dice at level 18, which sends you to Prince Bragnar for the bone dice and pays a Ball of Burlap Yarn carrying four charges of Snare. The Quellious Symbol chain and the Cold Iron quest both draw components from here as well.

04Dangers

The entrance area is the whole problem. The kobold density that makes it good hunting also makes it the worst train risk in the zone, and wandering kobolds add to fights on a regular basis. Crowd control or a real group is required, not optional.
The batlings are wizards and shamans, with no melee at all. Ten fixed 100% spawn points cluster them tightly in the middle of the zone. A pull that brings two is a pull against two casters at once, and Krode the Diviner sits at the bottom of their pit.
Diseased kobolds proc a disease that lasts a very long time. The wiki singles out the duration rather than the damage. Bring Cure Disease or plan to fight the rest of the room debuffed.
Two harm touches in the throne room bracket. King Gragnar hits for around 240 at level 24 and Prince Bragnar for 181 at level 18. Both are shadow knights, and Gragnar also lifetaps. At these levels a 240-point opener can remove a tank's cushion before the first heal lands.
A pack leader may see through invisibility. The wiki records this as an open question rather than a fact, with five spawn points at 33% each. Invisibility is the usual way past the entrance crush, so treat it as unreliable until confirmed.
Warlord Drrig's room has a one-way pit. The drop to the Batling Caves cannot be climbed back up. It is the intended route to Krode, but it puts you among the casters with no retreat.

05Getting there

  • The entrance sits in a small pool-filled room near the Paineel newbie area, on the way to the Hole entrance. The two dungeons share an approach.
  • Good characters can reach it. The obstacles are the skeletons in Toxxulia Forest near the Paineel entrance, and the Paineel newbie-area guards — which the wiki describes as very unaggressive and unlikely to act unless you walk close to them.
  • One documented hazard on the approach: a relatively high-level necromancer is sometimes found in the passageway and will attack any good character who passes in front of him. Be invisible if he is up.
  • You can bind right outside the entrance, which combined with the 6:40 spawn timer makes this a strong long-session zone.
  • The only other exit is west, where the kobolds have broken through into the Stonebrunt Mountains. That is also the sole route into Stonebrunt, so traffic passes through.
  • Succor and evacuate drop you at 748, −930, the zone line to Paineel.
