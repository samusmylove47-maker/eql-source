Clan Kolbok — a whole early career in one zone, and it counts as outdoors

Levels run 4 to 25 without leaving the zone. Come for the Cold Iron set off King Gragnar, the Crown of the Forlorn, and the Muglwump's seven no-drop pieces. The entrance is the whole problem — the kobold density that makes it good hunting is also the worst train risk here. Its experience modifier is joint second-highest of our 13 surveys, behind Kedge Keep.

Stonebrunt Mountains — and the only route into it, so traffic passes through

You can bind right outside the entrance. Good characters can reach it: the obstacles are the skeletons in Toxxulia Forest and the Paineel newbie guards, unaggressive unless you walk close. One documented hazard on the approach — a high-level necromancer is sometimes in the passageway and attacks any good character passing in front of him.

Five hazards

Cautions

  • The entrance area Wandering kobolds add to fights regularly, and this is where trains start. Crowd control or a real group is required, not optional.
  • The batlings are casters Wizards and shamans with no melee at all, on ten fixed spawn points clustered tightly. A pull that brings two is a pull against two casters.
  • Two harm touches in the throne room King Gragnar for around 240 at level 24, Prince Bragnar for 181 at 18. At these levels a 240-point opener removes a tank's cushion before the first heal lands.
  • Diseased kobolds The proc lasts a very long time — the duration is the problem, not the damage. Bring Cure Disease or fight the room debuffed.
  • A pack leader may see through invisibility Recorded as an open question, not a fact. Invisibility is the usual way past the entrance crush, so treat it as unreliable until someone confirms it.
