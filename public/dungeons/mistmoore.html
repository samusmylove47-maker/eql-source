The population changes at dawn and dusk

Levels run 20 to 45, with the roaming Dark Huntress above them. Come for the bard instruments, the focus items and the epic pelt. A train is what kills people, funnelling into the one corridor out. Everything below is D1 Awakened; D0 and D2 to D4 are unmeasured.

Five hazards

Cautions

  • Trains They start deep and funnel between the tower and the lake into the one corridor out. Shelter behind the lake, the tower or the zone line. Deep ones carry 500-plus nukes.
  • After a large train The returning line loiters near the entrance and pulling from it is lethal. Zone in invisible.
  • Invisibility is two spells Ordinary covers the day population, versus-undead the night, and the 20:00 swap changes which you need. The jail's secret door strips both.
  • Sitting at a repop tick Sitting gives level-1-equivalent aggro, and at 06:00 and 20:00 the population changes.
  • The approach Lesser Faydark is safe except Brownies north, Shadowed Men east, dark elves south.

What takes control away

Stuns here arrive as spells, not as melee. Stunning melee attacks were shrugged off and the spells landed anyway, so an immunity to stunning melee attacks does nothing about them. Screaming Terror locks you out of acting. It produces no fear behaviour whatsoever — no fleeing, nothing that behaves like fear — so despite the name it reads as a stun, and fear protection does not appear to touch it.
