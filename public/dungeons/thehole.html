A line marks the edge of the floor — a wall or a drop. Doors, locks and one-way drops are not marked, and a gap is as likely to be a ledge as a doorway. 18 of these 18 positions land on the drawn floor, which is worth stating because the coordinates and the geometry come from different sources and both have to be right for that to happen. 01Why you come here

  • The highest zone experience modifier in the game. ZEM 128, across a 39–56 band.
  • Earthshaker. A 37/70 two-hander with an Earthquake proc at 45, alongside Brell’s Girdle and the full Loam Encrusted caster set.
  • Levitate does not work here. Entering through the waterfall strips it and you fall for lethal damage at Master Yael’s feet, at the very bottom of the zone.
  • You cannot climb back onto the entry ledge. Once you are down, you are down.

Six epics route through this zone. The wiki's related-quest list names the Paladin, Ranger, Shadow Knight, Enchanter, Magician and Shaman epics, plus the Shiny Robe of the Underfoot. That is the real reason to learn the layout: sooner or later most classes have a reason to be down here.

04Dangers

Levitate does not work in The Hole. Entering through the waterfall in Paineel strips it, and you fall for lethal damage at Master Yael's feet at the very bottom of the zone. The same applies to descending the outer shaft around the castle.
You cannot climb back onto the entry ledge. Once you step off the first ledge you need either a teleport out or the key to escape. One workaround exists: walk backwards into the ledge while someone casts Gravity Flux on you and you land on top of it.
Master Yael sees through invisibility and death touches. If he is up, sneak and hide are the only stealth that work — otherwise leave by another route. He also casts Ice Comet.
Revenants and wanderers are extremely magic resistant, and the top-level ones cannot be mezzed or charmed at all. The wiki advises against mezzing wanderers for crowd control even where it lands. Rock golems are very magic resistant too. Between them, magic-based crowd control and nukes are the wrong plan for the Undead Tower.
Elemental healers Complete Heal at 40%. Channelers and harvesters both do it, and harvesters carry a magic nuke that lands for 500 or more. Elemental crusaders quick-heal themselves around 30% and need a stun to push them into flee mode at 20% — and since 28 July you cannot chain that stun.
Backstabs land for 348. Elemental capturers hit that figure; the weaker deceivers run around 248 with a maximum near 300. These are rogue-class mobs in a zone where most groups are watching for casters.

05Getting there

  • Two entrances, both inside Paineel. The waterfall hole is the fast way and the lethal way. The safe way is a small locked boulder in the area just past the newbie zone.
  • The boulder needs lockpicking, or the key — evil races can buy it from Rallia Hapera in Paineel at (941, 770) for a large sum, around 100 to 120 platinum.
  • Small races, wolf form and some illusions can squeeze through the rocks instead, though drowning is a real risk. Any race can also push through the corner of the door by working mouse-look rapidly; ducking, and ducking mid-jump, reportedly works often.
  • The portal room is room 16, reached through a hidden wall, and holds three pads: left to Paineel (Observation Tower), middle to Neriak Third Gate (top of the Cleric guild), right to Erudin. This is why the zone counts Neriak Third Gate and Erudin as adjacent.
  • Note that room 15, the Entry Observation Tower, has a locked door on its east end that the Hole Key does not open.
  • Succor and evacuate drop you at 640, −1050, the zone line to Paineel.
