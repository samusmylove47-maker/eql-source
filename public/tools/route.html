Where to go,
and what to take.

Pick your three classes and a starting level. It orders the ten surveyed zones by the level band each one covers and how much experience it returns, then lists what each drops that your trio can actually wear or wield.

Ten zones452 items filtered Every stop sourced

What this is not. It orders zones. It does not predict how long anything takes, because nothing we hold measures that — the zone experience modifier multiplies experience per kill, it is not a rate, and we have no kills-per-hour figure for any trio. An hours estimate here would be invented, so there is not one.

It also does not know your gear, your group, what is already camped, or whether you can survive the top of a band. If a stop is wrong for a reason like that, tell us — that is exactly the correction we cannot make from here.
