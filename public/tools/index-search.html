The Index

Every item and every named mob recorded across the 13 surveyed dungeons, in one searchable place. Ask it where something drops, what a zone holds for your classes, or which named you still have not met. 435 items and 232 named, each tied back to the survey it came from.

Every name here opens its own page. You can also browse the full A to Z of items or the A to Z of named mobs.

What this is and is not. It indexes the loot and named tables from our own surveys — so its coverage is exactly the 13 zones we have surveyed, not the whole game. 435 items, 440 rows: six drop in two zones. Stats are quoted as the survey records them, which means anything the survey flagged as uncertain is uncertain here too. Follow the zone link to read the surrounding context before you plan a night around a drop.
Looking for a gear upgrade check against your own inventory? EQL Tools has one that reads your inventory file and finds items that beat what you are wearing. That is a different job from this, done well, and there is no sense in us building a worse copy.
