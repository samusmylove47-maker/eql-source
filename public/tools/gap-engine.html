Gap engine.

Every other tracker here tells you what you have. This one reads your combat log and tells you what to change — as a difference against what you are already doing, never a score to hit. And it says out loud what it will not answer.

Reads your logYour log never leaves this machineNot yet live

This page is a preview and every number on it is invented. The engine runs, on real logs, in its own repository — it is not yet wired into this site, so there is no file input here rather than one that does nothing. A control that invites you to drop in a log and then does nothing would be the page lying to you, and this is a tool whose whole claim is that it refuses to overstate.

What would make it live: the engine ships as a bundle. When it does, this page reads your log in your browser and sends nothing — there is no server to send it to, and this site holds no logs at all, so it could not compute your figures even if it wanted to.

SYNTHETIC. Generated by running gapengine.py over an invented log, so the SHAPE is always exactly what the engine emits and cannot drift from it. The numbers carry no measurement and cannot be wrong.

01

Measured, and modelled, are shown as two different things

A page that mixes them invites you to trust the weaker one.

Measured comes out of the log, computed in your browser. A DPS figure always carries the window it was measured over: four shipped meters use four denominators, and the spread is more than double.

105.2DPS · engaged Engaged = damage over runs of hits with no gap above 15s, lasting 20s or more. Four shipped meters use four denominators; a DPS figure without its window is not a measurement.
0.32%Crit rate Counted from the log’s own crit lines, over the hits above.
328Hits counted Across 6 engagements. 15 killing blows are excluded from the rates: a killing blow is truncated by the target dying.
BalancedStance · inferred 48.5% even damage across 299 non-crit melee hits (killing blows excluded). Balanced prints ~50%, Offensive ~93%. Distance: 0.5 SE from Balanced, 15.4 SE from Offensive.

Counted, but not turned into a rate

A count with no denominator is not a rate, and the engine says so in the data rather than leaving a page to work it out.

Refusals 3

What it declined, with the reason and what would settle it. A tool that silently omits what it cannot do fails open: a short list reads as good news.

  • item.selectionsomeone else does this better

    Which obtainable item meets a stat floor is a catalogue question.

    What would settle it: eqlegendstools.com holds this and does it well. Link, do not clone.

  • worn.statsa log cannot see it

    A log does not show worn stats. AC, resists and worn ATK were not seen.

    What would settle it: The 50 Upgrades gear input, or a character-panel reading.

  • engaged_time.comparisonrefused, with no override

    Comparing how long two named characters were engaged is refused in all cases.

    What would settle it: Nothing. Hard refusal, ruled 30 August 2026.

03

What it saw, and what it assumed

Observed: crit rate, engaged time, resist counts, stance.
Assumed, and therefore a source of error: haste at cap, target mitigation, buff uptime.

Where it stops. Every delta is a difference against this character's own observed baseline. No absolute modelled figure appears in this document, by design - HANDOFF.md 21.3.
