The Quest Room

Build your trio. Track every test it owes.

In Legends these are not ordinary loot quests — they are the class unlock. Finishing every test on a class's line unlocks that class as a Primary Class option in your loadouts, tracked in game under /ach → Unlocked Potential. A Primary Class Unlock Token skips a whole line, and you get one free the first time you reach level 50.
Getting to the quest room costs nothing. Buy Efreeti's Key from the Key Master on Island 1 — it is free — then take the north teleport pad at roughly 1600, 520. The key stays on your character permanently.

Where this comes from

  • Turn-ins, all sixteen classes — eqprogression.com, Plane of Sky Quests / Class Unlocks, read 4 Aug 2026. This is the Legends-simplified structure: a wind rune plus one or two named items, rather than the three or four classic asked for.
  • Reward tooltips — eqlwiki.com/Plane_of_Sky, revision 151528 (28 Jun 2026). Verified for Bard, Beastlord, Berserker, Cleric, Druid, Enchanter, Magician, Monk, Necromancer and Paladin. Warrior tooltips are cross-read from eqlwiki.com/Warrior_Plane_of_Sky_Tests; that page's turn-ins are still classic and are not used here.
  • Flagged in red — Ranger, Rogue, Shadow Knight, Shaman and Wizard reward tooltips have no confirmed Legends-era stat block. A few partial lines come from the EQL Build Forge item snapshot dated 2 Aug 2026, and two shaman entries show classic numbers. The turn-ins for these classes are still Legends-current; only the stats are unverified. Read them in game and the gap closes.
  • Also unconfirmed — the sub-tester dialogue chain for Ranger, Rogue, Shadow Knight, Shaman and Wizard, and the Gem of Invigoration source on the warrior's Test of Smash.
  • A conflict worth knowing — the wiki's Dangers section says boss NPCs no longer death touch, while the island walkthroughs below it still describe death touch rotations on Keeper of Souls, Spiroc Lord, Bazzt Zzzt, Sister of the Spire and Eye of Veeshan. The Dangers line is the Legends-era edit; the walkthrough prose is inherited classic text. Assume no death touch, but do not stake a corpse recovery on it.
