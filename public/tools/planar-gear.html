Planar gear targets

Your trio can wear planar armour from all three of its classes, plus the two shared sets. That is five sets competing for every slot. Pick your three classes, choose what you are optimising for, and lock a target for each slot. 116 pieces across 18 sets, every figure from the item’s own record.

Every stat here is T3 — a classic-era item record, not confirmed against Legends piece by piece. That matters more in this tool than in a table: a ranking tells you what to chase, and the ranking is only as good as the records under it. The badge repeats on every card and on your locked total so it is never off screen when you decide.

Where these numbers come from. Every stat is read from that item’s own page on eqlwiki, mined 11 August 2026. A blank on the source is shown as nothing here, never as a zero. These are classic-era item records — they are the best available and they have not been confirmed against Legends piece by piece. Where a set looks short, it is short on the wiki:
One assumption, stated. The tool offers you any piece one of your three classes can wear. That follows how multiclass works elsewhere in the game, but we have not confirmed it for equipment specifically. If you find a piece your trio cannot equip, tell us and we will correct it.
Wrist counts twice. You wear two, so the tool lets you lock two. The planar sets carry no rings or earrings, so those slots are not offered rather than guessed at.
