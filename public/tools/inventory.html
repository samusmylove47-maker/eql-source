What have
I got?

Your inventory file is a wall of tab-separated text and nobody reads it. Paste it here and it becomes a list of what you are carrying, with everything this site knows about each piece attached — including 277 items whose drop source was measured in play rather than transcribed.

434 items catalogued 277 with a measured drop sourcenothing uploaded

Nothing leaves this page

The parsing happens in your browser. There is no upload and nothing is stored — an inventory names your character and lists what you own, and that stays yours. Close the tab and it is gone.

Type /outputfile inventory in game. It writes <Character>_<server>-Inventory.txt into your EverQuest Legends folder. Open it, select all, paste.

What this cannot tell you. The file carries names, IDs and counts — no stats at all. So there is no score here and no ranking of your gear: any such number would be invented rather than read. For what to chase next, the planar gear tool ranks the sets against your trio, and every set lists the pieces.
An unrecognised item marks a gap in this catalogue. It is mined from the dungeon surveys, so it covers the surveyed zones and nothing else. A row saying not catalogued here means that zone has not been written up yet.
On the number after the name. A drop arrives at the difficulty tier of the zone at minimum and occasionally one above, capping at +4 at Refined. Anything higher than +4 was made, not found. How difficulty decides that →
