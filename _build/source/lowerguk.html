Key to the plan

Where you are

Dead side runs north, live side runs south, and they overlap in the middle. The band where they interleave is real rather than a drawing error, and it is why people get lost here. A Y climbing past 800 is taking you into dead side and away from every exit.

The two crossings
The Elder Room and the bridge are the only places to cross between the sides. Live side is knights and shamans, with more wanderers and the maze. Dead side is knights and wizards, busier because there is no faction cost, and undead throughout — a gift to classes with undead specialisations
Two safe spots
The tunnel beneath the GIB Room, called Safe Hall. And a sharp L turn just above “C” on the bottom floor that no wanderer visits, where people regroup and even bind

Four ways in, and one way out nobody plans to use. Three Upper Guk entrances land close together on dead side, two of them needing a short swim; the fourth and deepest is the live side entrance. Behind the King’s Room is a one-way exit to Upper Guk beside the Innothule entrance — faster than evacuating, but it puts you past the toughest mob in the zone.

Reading a froglok

T5 — Classic EverQuest text. Legends mobs run three classes at once. Inherited, and wrong here: the classic guides say Lower Guk has no warriors, only knights, shamans and wizards. Legends mobs are multiclass and the roster below lists WAR on froglok after froglok. Read the kill order as a priority among the kits a mob is running, not as a statement about what it is.

Five hazards

Cautions

  • Invisibility is two spells and neither is safe Dead side holds living creatures that see through Invisibility versus Undead — the vampire bats above all, and a Deadly Black Widow waits in the Bedroom tunnel specifically to catch people running the wrong one. Live side gets undead from trains. Seeing through ordinary invisibility: the Evil Eye, the Huge Water Elemental, one basalt gargoyle, and every mob flagged undead.
  • Kill order Wizards first, then shamans, then knights, then pets. Wizards nuke hard with few hit points and are dead-side only; a Kor ghoul wizard in the Arch Magi area casts a 600-point nuke. Shamans carry pets and the nastier DoTs, Scourge and Venom of the Snake. Dead side mobs can also ghoulroot.
  • Live side costs you froglok faction That faction is what makes Upper Guk and much of live side safe to walk. Killing undead frogloks raises it back if you lose it by accident.
  • Dead side is a long way from any exit Most of it sits far past the entrances, and the wiki is blunt about it: bring an evacuator.
  • The live maze The wiki calls it deadlier than Cazic Thule’s, and rates the zone’s confusion, plus the lack of ready access to the deep areas, as its main defence. Hug the right wall to the Jail Room, then cut straight through to the Evil Eye.
