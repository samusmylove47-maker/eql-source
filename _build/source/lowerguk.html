01Why you come here

  • The best loot in the bracket. Flowing Black Silk Sash at Haste +21%, the Mithril Two-Handed Sword at +31%, and the Crown of the Froglok Kings.
  • Two warring sides that overlap in the middle. Dead side runs north, live side south, and the band where they interleave is why people get lost here.
  • The lowest experience modifier in the series. ZEM 86. You come for the loot, not the levelling.
  • Invisibility is two spells and neither is safe. Dead side holds living creatures that see through Invisibility versus Undead; live side gets undead from trains.

02Know where you are

Dead side runs north, live side runs south, and they overlap in the middle. The band where they interleave is real rather than a drawing error, and it is why people get lost here. The Elder Room and the bridge are the two places you can cross between them.

03Reading a froglok

A froglok's clan name gives you its level. Learn the ladder and you can con the whole zone by name. Every melee clan has a caster counterpart of matching difficulty.

There are no warriors in Lower Guk — only knights, shamans and wizards. Live side has knights and shamans; dead side has knights and wizards. Kill order is fixed: wizards first (they nuke hard and have few hit points), then shamans, then knights, then pets. Dead side mobs can also ghoulroot.

04The two sides

Live · 24–49

Frogloks

Knights and shamans. Hunting here seriously damages froglok faction, which many players want intact to travel Upper Guk safely.

More wanderers than dead side, and the maze. If you lose faction by accident, killing undead frogloks raises it back.

Dead · 24–49

Froglok ghouls

Knights and wizards. Busier, because there is no faction cost. Undead — a gift for classes with undead specialisations.

Most of it is very far from any exit. Bring an evacuator.

Both

The maze is the defence

The wiki's assessment: the zone's main defence is how confusing it is, plus the lack of ready access to the deep areas.

Hug the right wall through the live maze to the jail room, then cut straight through to the evil eye.

Invisibility is unreliable here, and the reason is subtle. The live/dead split is not absolute. Dead side holds many living creatures that see straight through Invisibility versus Undead — the vampire bats most of all, and a Deadly Black Widow waits in the tunnel by the Bedroom specifically to catch people running the wrong one. Live side occasionally holds undead from trains. Several mobs see through ordinary invisibility too: the Evil Eye, the Huge Water Elemental, one of the basalt gargoyles, and every mob flagged undead.
Four ways in, one way out that nobody plans to use. Three Upper Guk entrances land close together on dead side — two need a short swim, the dry one is nearest Guk's front entrance. The fourth, deepest into Upper Guk, is the live side entrance. Behind the King's Room there is a one-way zone exit to Upper Guk beside the Innothule entrance: faster than evacuating, but it puts you past the toughest mob in the zone.

07Dangers

Invisibility is two spells and neither is safe. Dead side holds living creatures that see through Invisibility versus Undead — vampire bats above all, and a Deadly Black Widow that waits in the Bedroom tunnel specifically for people running the wrong one. Live side gets undead from trains. Independently see-invis: the Evil Eye, the Huge Water Elemental, one basalt gargoyle, and every mob flagged undead.
Live side costs you froglok faction. That faction is what makes Upper Guk and much of live side safe to walk. If you lose it by accident, killing undead frogloks raises it back.
Dead side is a long way from any exit. The wiki is blunt: bring an evacuator. Most of it sits far past the entrances.
Shamans and wizards are the whole problem. Shamans carry pets and the nastier DoTs — Scourge, Venom of the Snake. Wizards nuke hard with few hit points and are dead-side only. Kill wizards first, then shamans, then knights, then pets. A Kor ghoul wizard in the Arch Magi area casts a 600-point nuke.
The live maze is deadlier than Cazic Thule's. The wiki says so directly. Hug the right wall to the Jail Room, then cut straight through to the Evil Eye, which is the hub for everything else. The Elder Room also has a pit trap that can kill you — though falling in gets you a clean solo fight with the elder.
Two genuinely safe spots. The tunnel beneath the GIB Room is called Safe Hall. And just above "C" on the bottom floor there is a sharp L turn no wanderer visits — people regroup and even bind there.

08Getting there

  • Upper Guk is the only adjacent zone, with four entrances down. Three land close together on dead side — two need a short swim, the dry one is nearest Guk's front entrance.
  • The live side entrance is the deepest into Upper Guk and least used.
  • Behind the King's Room is a one-way exit to Upper Guk beside the Innothule entrance. Faster than evacuating, but it runs you past the toughest mob in the zone.
  • You must be level 5 to zone in.
  • Succor and evacuate drop you at 1197, −217, the Upper Guk zone line between the Bedroom and the Waterfall.
