01Reading the map

Dead side runs north, live side runs south — but they overlap in the middle. Filled markers are Live, hollow rings are Dead. The band where they interleave is real, not a drawing error, and it is why people get lost here. The Elder Room and the bridge are the two places you can cross between them.
  • /loc returns Y, X, Z. +Y is north (up), +X is west (left).
  • Rising Y takes you deeper into dead side. Falling Y takes you into live side and the king.
  • A froglok's clan name is its level. Guk > Bok/Jin > Dar/Kor > Zol/Yun > Wan/Urd > Vis/Tal > Shin/Tsu, from 45–49 down to 24–31.
  • Dots are surveyed /loc. Passages are schematic.

02The map

Four named are not plotted because they have no coordinates. The Ghoul Lord is marked by his two Guk guards, the Arch Magi by his jin-wizard placeholder. Slaythe the Slayer (a dead end just north of the Jail Room) and Raster of Guk (Elder Room, upper alcove by the cauldron) have no recorded position at all.

03Getting through

StepDirections
Pick an entranceFour from Upper Guk. Three land together on dead side — two need a short swim, the dry one is nearest Guk's front entrance. The fourth, deepest into Upper Guk, is the live side entrance and is least used.
Through the live mazeHug the right wall all the way to the Jail Room, then cut straight through. That lands you at the Evil Eye. The wiki calls this maze deadlier than Cazic Thule's.
From the Evil EyeThe live hub. Facing out of the cubby: left → Live Keep. Right → Jail Room or Noble Room. Straight → Elder Room, or on to dead side.
Toward the kingThrough the gateway in the Noble Room. Past King Prep, then the King Room. Behind the king is a one-way zone exit to Upper Guk beside the Innothule entrance — the emergency door.
Live → deadA small tower from the Elder Room leads across. You can pull dead-side mobs from there.
Into dead side properMost expeditions gather at the Bedroom to buff and invis, then move out. From the bridge: fall off left for a quick drop to the bottom floor; turn right to go down to live side.
  • Two safe spots. The tunnel beneath the GIB Room is Safe Hall. And just above "C" on the bottom floor there is a sharp L turn no wanderer visits — people regroup and even bind there.
  • Live side entrance shortcut: falling off the bridge on the left drops you to the Shin Knight Water Room.
  • The Store Room is off to the side and up a ladder — easy to walk past.

06Caveats

  • The page contradicts itself on the respawn timer. The infobox says 9:28 (corrected 10 July); the Monsters section still says 28 minutes. This map follows the infobox — and one NPC note updated to "9m 27s" in the same pass corroborates it.
  • Four named have no coordinates — the Ghoul Lord, the Arch Magi, Slaythe the Slayer and Raster of Guk. The first two are located by guard and placeholder; the last two are not plotted.
  • Spawn percentages predate the 28 July launch pass, which removed placeholders. Treat them as floors.
  • Passages are schematic. The maze in particular cannot be drawn honestly from coordinates alone — use the right-wall rule in §03.
