01Revamp status, and a source warning

Treated on launch day, 28 July 2026 — placeholders removed and maximum respawn times lowered, alongside The Hole, Nagafen's Lair, Lower Guk, Lair of the Splitpaw and Castle Mistmoore. Because the wiki page predates that patch by ten weeks, every spawn percentage and respawn timer in section 04 is a pre-patch figure and should now be better. Several named here were on percentage placeholders, so this is the zone in the launch pass with the most to gain.

The two numbers that matter

  • ZEM 128 (171%) — but it came down from 150. The Warrens is the only zone in this series whose modifier was reduced by the 23 June rebalance rather than raised, and it still lands tied with The Hole at the top of the table. A 4–25 zone sharing the highest experience modifier in the game with a 39–56 zone is an unusual piece of tuning and the single strongest argument for levelling here.
  • A 6 minute 40 second zone spawn timer. Faster than Crushbone's 9:00 and roughly three times faster than Blackburrow's 22:00, though still slower than Befallen's 4:27. Combined with the ZEM, a named-clearing loop here pays unusually well for the bracket.
The zone is flagged as outdoors. The wiki calls this odd, and it is, but it means Spirit of Wolf, Harmony and other outdoor-only spells work inside the dungeon. Movement speed and pacification in a cave network is a real structural advantage no other dungeon in this series offers.
Faction runs both ways here. Killing kobolds raises standing with both the Heretics and the Erudites — the two factions that are otherwise opposed on Odus. For a character making Odus a long-term home that is a rare double, and it is why evil Erudites can stay near their home city for the first twenty levels.

Rooms 2, 7–10 · 6–15

Entrance & prison

The east end. Heavy kobold density, which is the best low-level hunting in the zone and also its worst train risk. Lower-level players pull to the entrance from two directions.

The prison holds captured Erudites, a Kejekan and Koajin, a Kerran craftsman. Two of the zone's three quests start here.

Rooms 1, 3–6, 11 · 18–25

City & throne room

Where the gear is. King Gragnar and High Shaman Drogik share the throne room; Prince Bragnar, Warlord Drrig, Krode and Roggik ring it.

The batlings are the real hazard — their group is entirely wizards and shamans.

Rooms 12–14 · 14–24

Bat caves & the west end

Giant cave bats, the Cave Bat Lord, Huntmaster Furgrl and The Mighty Bear Paw — a Walrus Man, oddly, this far from ice.

Beyond here the kobolds have broken through into the Stonebrunt Mountains, the zone's only other exit.

The bats do not play by kobold rules. Giant cave bats are not social with kobolds or with each other, and they flee at low health. The Cave Bat Lord is stranger still: he is classed as a kobold, not a bat, and none of the nearby bats will come to his aid. That makes him a clean single pull at level 22 in a zone otherwise full of assists.

06Dangers

The entrance area is the whole problem. The kobold density that makes it good hunting also makes it the worst train risk in the zone, and wandering kobolds add to fights on a regular basis. Crowd control or a real group is required, not optional.
The batlings are wizards and shamans, with no melee at all. Ten fixed 100% spawn points cluster them tightly in the middle of the zone. A pull that brings two is a pull against two casters at once, and Krode the Diviner sits at the bottom of their pit.
Diseased kobolds proc a disease that lasts a very long time. The wiki singles out the duration rather than the damage. Bring Cure Disease or plan to fight the rest of the room debuffed.
Two harm touches in the throne room bracket. King Gragnar hits for around 240 at level 24 and Prince Bragnar for 181 at level 18. Both are shadow knights, and Gragnar also lifetaps. At these levels a 240-point opener can remove a tank's cushion before the first heal lands.
A pack leader may see through invisibility. The wiki records this as an open question rather than a fact, with five spawn points at 33% each. Invisibility is the usual way past the entrance crush, so treat it as unreliable until confirmed.
Warlord Drrig's room has a one-way pit. The drop to the Batling Caves cannot be climbed back up. It is the intended route to Krode, but it puts you among the casters with no retreat.

07Getting there

  • The entrance sits in a small pool-filled room near the Paineel newbie area, on the way to the Hole entrance. The two dungeons share an approach.
  • Good characters can reach it. The obstacles are the skeletons in Toxxulia Forest near the Paineel entrance, and the Paineel newbie-area guards — which the wiki describes as very unaggressive and unlikely to act unless you walk close to them.
  • One documented hazard on the approach: a relatively high-level necromancer is sometimes found in the passageway and will attack any good character who passes in front of him. Be invisible if he is up.
  • You can bind right outside the entrance, which combined with the 6:40 spawn timer makes this a strong long-session zone.
  • The only other exit is west, where the kobolds have broken through into the Stonebrunt Mountains. That is also the sole route into Stonebrunt, so traffic passes through.
  • Succor and evacuate drop you at 748, −930, the zone line to Paineel.
