A gated descent through three stacked maps

Levels run 8 to 35, tuned for 15 to 30. Come for the Kitchen Toolbelt, off a level 22 named near the front and behind no keys, and for the four-key chain down to Najena. Mist elementals end runs here — ordinary weapons cannot hurt them and they go invisible seconds after spawning. No session in this zone has been logged, so everything below is read rather than measured.

Key to the plan

Where you are

Three maps stacked, and two of them do not connect to each other. You enter at X 856, and deeper means X falls — a climbing X means you are walking back out.

The three maps
Main, off the zone line: front halls, caves, goblin camps, Najena, Drelzna, the Blood Artist, the Crystal Room. Underground A, two-way by stair A: BoneCracker, Trazdon, Officer Grush, A Visiting Priestess, the guard captain. Underground B, by stair B past Drelzna or a one-way fall through C or D: the prison, Moosh, Linara Parlone, the injured halfling, Ekeros, Rathyl

No wizard port lands here. Druids take Ring of Lavastorm, then Circle of Lavastorm later; two wiki pages disagree on the levels. Wizards port to Nektulos and run east and north.

Five hazards

Cautions

  • Mist elementals Sixteen elemental spawn points exist and none are published, so expect one anywhere, a named camp included. Ordinary weapons cannot hurt a mist elemental and it goes invisible seconds after spawning — See Invisible to target it, magic damage to kill it.
  • Rathyl reincarnate A level 30 skeleton with a 35-damage frost proc, spawning instantly on Rathyl’s corpse and on you before the loot window.
  • Moosh, and Rivervale Attacking him pulls an injured halfling out of the cell, and killing that halfling tanks every Rivervale faction. He hits well above his level.
  • Two pit traps on the upper level They drop you into the prison, and climbing out is harder than falling in. The Guard’s Keyring is the way out; some take them deliberately as a shortcut to Rathyl.
  • The front halls “A skeleton” is not a plain skeleton — the level 21 to 22 ones here are the most dangerous in the zone, and a large skeleton cons deceptively. Rooms hold two or three inside each other’s aggro radius.

Read off the wiki’s Dangers prose, Project 1999 text T5: the positions hold, the estimates of what a mob is and how many players it needs do not. Two more from it — chain-stunning stopped working on 28 July, stun immunity now outlasting the stun, and nothing here death-touches, unconfirmed.

Two registers

What drops

The wiki lists 80 items dropping here. Weapon lines read damage / delay. As of 29 July every roll is gated to your difficulty tier or better, with a diminishing chance at higher tiers. Rare, common and uncommon are the wiki’s words, not measured rates.

Journeyman’s Boots — the sources disagree, and they drop. The eqlwiki item record names Drelzna, and the room 5 map entry lists her dropping them with full stats. One line of prose says she “used to spawn the Journeyman Boots (but not as of ? era)” — word for word Allakhazam, so classic text. Two things favour the drop: an active editor expanded that map section twice in July, and the 23 June patch note ends on a developer joke about their own boots. They drop from Drelzna, and there is no quest, reported by a player on 14 August 2026 rather than read from a log. The register entry →

Three of the four best items here are NEC WIZ MAG ENC only, and the magicians carry mage focus quest items besides. A melee-heavy group levels well and watches the loot go elsewhere.

Damask Armor is an eleven-piece set, NEC WIZ MAG ENC and all races, off ordinary a magician spawns at ten recorded points between the entry gate and Rathyl’s wing. No keys, no named, and it drops in Befallen too, so pieces from either zone combine.

The keys run guard captain, BoneCracker, Rathyl, Drelzna, each dropping the one to the next. All four persist through logout, so the chain need not be run in a sitting; the wiki’s prose calls two No Rent and the item records disagree. A small enough character slips the bars and skips keys 1 and 2.

The Necromancer and Magician epics route through here, with The Mystic Cloak and the Torch of Alna, Shovel of Ponz, Stein of Ulissa and Broom of Trilon chains. Only one quest starts here: The Visiting Priestess, minimum level 30, rewarding the Blued Two-Handed Hammer, on through Mistmoore Castle, Neriak Commons, Neriak Third Gate and Solusek’s Eye.
