The orc keep north of Kelethin — nine-minute respawns and a rebuilt loot tier

Levels 4 to 22, from pawns at the zone line to Emperor Crush in his tower. Come for the 14 July loot tier: Crown of Crush, the Dwarven Ringmail line and Headsplitter. Trains are what kill people, every five to ten minutes, through the zone line. Bridge pathing is broken.

Key to the plan

Where you are

Open ground west, the keep east, the slaver workings south.

A path between two pyres
The entrance. North from Kelethin's northernmost lift, or right out of Felwithe along the zone border. Circle of Butcherblock and the translocator land within a run
A hilltop crowded with orcs
Trainer Hill. Six or seven spawns, fast enough that you never pull from elsewhere
A building in front of a tree
The safe spot at the Slaver Caves, left on zone-in. Right wall instead for the Pit
A bridge to the castle
The Wall camp is in front of it. Pull the roamers outside first
A tented area at the back
Tents. Shares a four-spawn crevice with the Slaver cave, and the corpse run is long
Five camps
Trainer Hill 8 to 13. Slaver Caves 5 to 8, the Pit 12 to 16. Tents 8 to 14. Wall 14 to 18. Throne Room 17 to 22, where Emperor Crush and Ambassador DVinn are pulled from

Four hazards

Cautions

  • Trains Every five to ten minutes, through the zone line: legionnaires, oracles, an emissary, sometimes Emperor Crush himself. Learn the paths and stay off them.
  • Broken pathing Bridges especially, toward the slaver camp and outside the keep. An orc that goes for a swim routes back behind the castle through the whole zone.
  • The Royal Guard Invisible until it attacks, behind the throne on the way up to Emperor Crush.
  • Faction Killing slaves damages faction with their home city. Retlon Brenclog costs Ak'Anon.
