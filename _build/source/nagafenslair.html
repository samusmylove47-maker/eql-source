Sol B — nothing here can be hidden from, and the lava is not scenery

Levels run 25 to 55, kobold camps above and the fire giant castle below. Come for Djarn's focus items, the Mithril Breastplate, and a Cloak of Shadows that drops off a level 25 mob. Nothing here can be hidden from — everything sees invisibility and greater kobolds see hide, so a corpse run is a fight. The lava is a damage source, not scenery.

The areas near the Sol A zone lines are the safe ground: you can pull there and zone out if a pull goes wrong. Pull points and escape lines elsewhere are unrecorded.

Five hazards

Cautions

  • Nothing can be hidden from Everything sees invisibility; greater kobolds see hide as well. A corpse run is a fight, not a stealth problem.
  • The lava Falling in hurts badly and there is not always a way out. A pit trap into lava and a block crusher trap are marked on the wiki map. Fire-resist gear is worth carrying.
  • Stuns and stacked DoTs Sonic bat melee procs a long single-target stun. The bugs travel in packs and carry poison and disease; the noxious spider's DoT alone does 1200.
  • Fire giants break the normal rules They cannot be mesmerised, resist every spell cast from outside melee range, and aggro each other through walls. A feign pull is essential.
  • The Efreeti area has no exit Imp protectors ring Djarn and the pathing is difficult: there is no way out but to evacuate. The tunnels elsewhere wind, and getting lost is its own hazard.
