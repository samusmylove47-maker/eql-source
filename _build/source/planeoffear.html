01Why you come here

  • Four of the seven planar armour slots. Fear drops Chest, Wrist, Hands and Feet. Hate drops Head, Arms, Wrist and Legs. If you want a full set you are visiting both, and Wrist is the one slot either plane can give you.
  • A 110% ZEM with the raid bosses moved out. The seven plane bosses stopped spawning in the open world and in personal instances on 16 June, so the zone you walk into now is trash and minibosses at a raised experience rate. That is the whole basis of the 46+ levelling reputation, and it is a developer change rather than a community theory.
  • Phoboplasm drops any planar armour — including Hate-only pieces. It is the one mob in either plane that ignores the group split.
  • Come for the armour, not the bosses. The five raid targets are instance-only and almost nothing published about them describes Legends. Section 02 is not throat-clearing; on this zone it is the most useful thing we can tell you.

02What the sources are, and why it matters here

We check the revision history of every page we lean on. On Fear that check comes back worse than on any zone we have surveyed.

The eqlwiki Plane of Fear page was created on 9 March 2026 by P99Wiki>RibbonRMX — an import from the Project 1999 wiki, four months before EverQuest Legends existed. The Cazic Thule page is the same story: imported>CrazyPro, 29 March 2026. Both were edited afterwards by named editors, and none of those edits describe a measurement.

The tell is not subtle. The published Cazic Thule strategy says, word for word:

It’s recommended to have several dozen melee and a dozen+ healers.

Legends caps a raid at 8. The same page discusses the Velious-era revamp of the zone and chain-CH healing rotations. This is not a stale page. It is a correct description of a different game, and following it here is not possible.

So this survey badges nearly everything, and the badges mean what they say. Where a figure is ours or the developers’, it prints plain.

03Getting in, and not getting out

One way in, no ordinary way out, and a welcome party that sees invisibility.

  1. The Feerrott, spectre caves one-way portal level 46+ → you arrive in the south-west
  2. The portal is guarded see-invis, immediate do not zone in alone
  3. Leaving means Gate, a port, or dying or the Temple exit portal the Temple route is worse

Succor and evacuate put you at −1139, 1282, the south-west corner by the zone-in. That is your reset button and it is worth knowing before you need it. There is a race line at the Feerrott portal. T5

The imported page recommends a charged Leatherfoot Raider Skullcap and advises against binding in the zone. Both are classic-era advice about a classic-era corpse run, and we have not confirmed either applies. Treat them as leads, not instructions.

04Your armour, slot by slot

Every ordinary mob in Fear drops planar armour for one slot. Find your slot, then hunt any mob on that row.

The class-group split is gone, and one mob moved. A Legends-era guild loot guide lists these mobs merged rather than divided into two class groups, and puts a turmoil toad at Wrist where the imported table has it at Hands. Lustrous Russet and Midnight Clad are the two shared sets, and this is now confirmed from the item records themselves. Every Russet piece reads WAR CLR PAL RNG SHD BRD ROG SHM and every Midnight Clad piece reads DRU MNK ROG NEC WIZ MAG ENC BST — chain and plate against cloth and leather, with Rogue the only class on both lists. Between them they cover all sixteen classes. Phoboplasms drop Midnight Clad; haunted chests in Hate drop Lustrous Russet. C

A gap we are not smoothing over. a decrepit warder appears in the armour table as a Group 1 Wrist drop and then appears nowhere else on the source page — no level, no abilities, no sees-invis. A whole mob type is missing its record.

06The five raid targets

Instance-only since 16 June. The roster below is confirmed by the developers; the numbers beside it are not.

The patch note of 16 June names them: Fright, Terror, Dread, a dracoliche and Cazic Thule no longer spawn in the open world or in personal instances of the Plane of Fear. That list matches the wiki’s roster exactly, so who is here is settled. Every published statistic about them is classic EverQuest.

One boss mechanic here is not classic text. The hotfix of 24 June 2026 reads: “Cazic-Thule now properly calls for his minions when he is engaged in an instance of the Plane of Fear.” That is the developers confirming zone-wide social aggro, in a Legends instance. It is the only thing on this page about a boss fight that we can state flatly.

Every published respawn timer for these five is about something that no longer happens. 72 hours for the golems, 7 days for the dracoliche — those describe an open-world spawn the 16 June patch removed. What governs access now is the raid instance and the weekly loot lockout, which resets Tuesdays at 8AM PST.

Raid bosses in Legends run three classes from D3, and every one of these is listed with a single class. That alone dates the table. Which class kits attach to which raid boss at D3 and above is unpublished by anyone, and it is the largest gap on this site.

07Levelling, plat and AA at 46+

The reputation is real and it has a mechanical basis, but nobody — including us — has published a rate.

WhatWhere it comes from
ZEM 82.5, or 110% Zone infobox. The 23 June patch note also raised instanced Hate, Fear and Sky to match the open world, so an instance costs you no experience.
Bosses out of the open world 16 June patch note. The zone is trash and minibosses now.
Loot scales with difficulty 29 July hotfix: drops match the tier you are in, with a diminishing chance to roll higher.
A lockout kill is not a wasted kill 28 July patch note: killing a raid boss while locked still gives one guaranteed drop from its unique table.
AA gain is reported as strong One creator farming the Plane of Hate reports a single trash kill giving 9% of an AA. C That is Hate, not Fear, and it is one reading with no level or difficulty stated.

We will not print a plat-per-hour or AA-per-hour figure until we have measured one. No source we have found publishes either for this zone at any difficulty. That is the opening, not the excuse: it is the first thing our own logs could settle.

One creator who has farmed both notes only that “the ads in fear can kind of get annoying” compared with Hate. C Hate also carries the higher ZEM — 86 against 82.5. If you are choosing purely on experience, Hate is ahead.
