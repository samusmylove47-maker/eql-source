The Ruins of Old Paineel — a shaft you cannot climb back out of

Levels run 39 to 56 and deepen as you descend. Come for the Loam Encrusted and Withered Leather sets, the AC 20 Imbued Granite Spauldors off ordinary trash, and the six epics that route through here. Levitate does not work — entering through the waterfall strips it and drops you at Master Yael's feet for lethal damage. Its experience modifier is @@ZEM_RANK@@.

Evil races can buy the key from Rallia Hapera in Paineel at 941, 770, for roughly 100 to 120 platinum. Small races, wolf form and some illusions squeeze through the rocks instead, at the risk of drowning; ducking through the corner of the door reportedly works for any race.

Five hazards

Cautions

  • The shaft is one-way Levitate does not work here. The waterfall strips it and you fall for lethal damage at the bottom; the outer shaft is the same. You cannot climb back onto the entry ledge without a teleport, the key, or Gravity Flux cast on you as you walk backwards into it.
  • Master Yael sees through invisibility And death touches. While he is up, sneak and hide are the only stealth that work.
  • Magic crowd control is the wrong plan Revenants and wanderers are extremely magic resistant and the top-level ones cannot be mezzed or charmed at all. Rock golems resist heavily too.
  • Elemental healers complete heal at 40% Channelers and harvesters both, and harvesters nuke for 500 or more. Crusaders quick-heal near 30% and need a stun at 20% — and since 28 July that stun cannot be chained.
  • The trash backstabs Elemental capturers and deceivers are rogues in a zone where groups watch for casters. @@H_BACKSTAB@@ — @@H_BS_RATIO@@ as hard from behind as from the front.
