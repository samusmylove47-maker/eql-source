01Revamp status, and a source warning

Read this before trusting any prose in this guide's sources. The eqlwiki page for The Hole was created on 12 August 2024 as a direct import from the Project 1999 wiki, and has changed by a net −74 bytes in the two years since. Its infobox and its NPC and item tables are Legends data — they carry EQL item IDs, EQL focus effects and the rebalanced ZEM. Its narrative sections are classic EverQuest text. Everything in this plate's dangers and travel sections descends from that classic prose and is therefore geometry-grade only: layout and mechanics that never changed. Levels, drops and coordinates below come from the structured tables.
Treated on launch day, 28 July 2026. The patch note reads: placeholders removed from and maximum respawn times lowered in The Hole, Nagafen's Lair, Lower Guk, Lair of the Splitpaw, The Warrens and Castle Mistmoore, closing with a note that more dungeon treatments will follow.
ZEM 128 (171%), raised from 100 in the 23 June rebalance. The wiki infobox still shows the old value struck through beside the new one. This is the highest confirmed dungeon modifier in the series — a hair above Splitpaw's 128 (170%) — and it runs 49% above Lower Guk and Nagafen's Lair, which both sit at 86 (115%). For characters in the high 40s and 50s this is where the experience is.

Three launch-week changes that reshape this zone specifically

  • Charm is capped at 30 seconds. The 28 July patch caps loss-of-player-control spells, NPC charm included, at 30 seconds duration. The wiki's NPC table calls the elemental capturer an amazing enchanter and bard pet and the deceiver an excellent group pet, and classic guides to this zone treat charm as the defining tactic here. That advice is now obsolete: a charmed elemental is a 30-second cooldown, not a tank.tactic change
  • Chain-stunning no longer works. Stunned players and NPCs both now gain short-term immunity to further stuns that outlasts the current stun. The elemental crusader's counter — stun it at 20% to force flee mode — still works once, but it cannot be repeated back-to-back.
  • Loot rolls follow difficulty tier as of 29 July: you roll your tier or better, with a diminishing chance at higher tiers. Every drop source in section 05 is tier-gated on top of its own rate, and the raid change means killing a boss while holding a loot lockout still yields one guaranteed item from its unique table.

The Undead Tower looks crowded because it is vertical. Ten of the twenty plotted positions fall inside a box roughly 170 game units square in the north-west. That is not a plotting artefact — the tower is a stack of tightly packed rooms, so its named separate on Z, which a plan view cannot show. Read that cluster as one column, not one floor. The same is true in miniature at the bottom centre, where High Scale Kirn, the mimic, Kejar and Schnozz sit within about 40 units of each other.
Most named here are not on the plate at all, and that is deliberate. The elemental line — Bejeweled elemental, Rocksoul, Stonegrinder Minion, Gibartik, Muck covered elemental, Stonesoul the Unmoving, The Stone Caller — can spawn at the location of any elemental mob, and more than one copy of the same named can be up at once. The undead line behaves the same way off the revenant and wanderer pools. A named undead cannot take an elemental point or the reverse, but within its own pool it is everywhere. Plotting those would be inventing coordinates, so they live in the roster instead.

03Structure — two halves and a pit

City & castle · 38–54

Elementals

Most of the zone. Elemental warriors, crusaders, deceivers, capturers, channelers, harvesters, visiers and wizards, mixed with ratman warriors and rock golems.

Many are casters, some summon, and wanderers roam throughout. The wiki's assessment: at least one full group of level 50+ is necessary to do well.

Undead Tower · 43–56

Erudite ghosts

No elementals at all in this tower. Tightly packed rooms, vertical layout, mostly necromancers and shadow knights, with fallen erudites at the graveyards and crypt.

Generally considered the most dangerous area in the zone because of how many aggressive mobs arrive with each pull.

The Pit · 52–56

Master Yael

The very bottom of the shaft. Yael stands before a locked door with no known key, presumed to lead to the Plane of Underfoot.

He death touches. Nortlav the Scalekeeper stands beside him. Raid instance only.

Two class lines are outsized here. Many of the monsters count as summoned, which makes the Dismiss Summoned line significantly more effective. Most of the remainder count as undead, which favours clerics and paladins. Between the two, a well-built group has an answer for nearly everything in the zone.

06Dangers

Levitate does not work in The Hole. Entering through the waterfall in Paineel strips it, and you fall for lethal damage at Master Yael's feet at the very bottom of the zone. The same applies to descending the outer shaft around the castle.
You cannot climb back onto the entry ledge. Once you step off the first ledge you need either a teleport out or the key to escape. One workaround exists: walk backwards into the ledge while someone casts Gravity Flux on you and you land on top of it.
Master Yael sees through invisibility and death touches. If he is up, sneak and hide are the only stealth that work — otherwise leave by another route. He also casts Ice Comet.
Revenants and wanderers are extremely magic resistant, and the top-level ones cannot be mezzed or charmed at all. The wiki advises against mezzing wanderers for crowd control even where it lands. Rock golems are very magic resistant too. Between them, magic-based crowd control and nukes are the wrong plan for the Undead Tower.
Elemental healers Complete Heal at 40%. Channelers and harvesters both do it, and harvesters carry a magic nuke that lands for 500 or more. Elemental crusaders quick-heal themselves around 30% and need a stun to push them into flee mode at 20% — and since 28 July you cannot chain that stun.
Backstabs land for 348. Elemental capturers hit that figure; the weaker deceivers run around 248 with a maximum near 300. These are rogue-class mobs in a zone where most groups are watching for casters.

07Getting there — and the portal room

  • Two entrances, both inside Paineel. The waterfall hole is the fast way and the lethal way. The safe way is a small locked boulder in the area just past the newbie zone.
  • The boulder needs lockpicking, or the key — evil races can buy it from Rallia Hapera in Paineel at (941, 770) for a large sum, around 100 to 120 platinum.
  • Small races, wolf form and some illusions can squeeze through the rocks instead, though drowning is a real risk. Any race can also push through the corner of the door by working mouse-look rapidly; ducking, and ducking mid-jump, reportedly works often.
  • The portal room is room 16, reached through a hidden wall, and holds three pads: left to Paineel (Observation Tower), middle to Neriak Third Gate (top of the Cleric guild), right to Erudin. This is why the zone counts Neriak Third Gate and Erudin as adjacent.
  • Note that room 15, the Entry Observation Tower, has a locked door on its east end that the Hole Key does not open.
  • Succor and evacuate drop you at 640, −1050, the zone line to Paineel.
