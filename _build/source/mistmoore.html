01Revamp status, and a claim worth correcting

Treated on launch day, 28 July 2026 — placeholders removed and maximum respawn times lowered, alongside The Hole, Nagafen's Lair, Lower Guk, Lair of the Splitpaw and The Warrens. Every spawn percentage in section 04 is a pre-patch figure. This zone had unusually punishing rates — an avenging caitiff at 10%, a glyphed warder at 4%, a deathly usher at 8% — so it has as much to gain from the placeholder removal as any zone in the pass.
The wiki's prose calls this "arguably some of the best experience for any zone in the game." In EverQuest Legends that is no longer true. The rebalanced ZEM is 97 (129%), up slightly from 90. That puts Mistmoore below Crushbone, Befallen, Blackburrow and Najena, which all sit at 119 (159%), and far below The Hole and The Warrens at 128 (171%). It beats only Lower Guk and Nagafen's Lair at 86 (115%). The classic claim reflects pre-Legends tuning and a much older experience formula. Come here for the loot and the atmosphere, not for the rate.

The mechanic that defines the zone

The castle repopulates twice a day in game time — 06:00 and 20:00. At dawn the undead give way to dark elf nobles and musicians; at dusk the vampires and werewolves rise and replace many of the daytime creatures. Any monster still alive respawns anyway, and anything killed repops. The zone's 22-minute timer resets at both events.

Three consequences. First, invisibility is only half a solution — ordinary invisibility hides you from the daytime population, invisibility versus undead only from the night population, and the swap means the correct spell changes twice a day. Second, a camp cleared at 19:55 is full again at 20:00, with a different roster. Third, and worst: sitting gives level-1-equivalent aggro, so being seated at a repop tick can hand you the entire room.

Rooms 1–7, 13 · 24–37

Outer grounds

Graveyard, pond, pit, canyon, jail, courtyard, fountain and the tower. Gargoyles and familiars here, plus the two best solo-safe named.

This is where a careful group hunts. The zone edge is close, so anything that goes wrong can be answered by zoning.

Rooms 8–12, 14–23 · 27–40

The castle

Servants, librarians, gypsies, nobles, the throne room and the bath. Nearly every named worth killing lives in here, and six of them are on Level 2.

Deep castle trains can carry mobs that land 500 or more damage with a single spell.

Triggered · 45

Black Dire

A level 45 wolf elemental shadow knight that spawns at the end of the canyon only when an advisor is killed.

Hits in the 200s, can quad, and harm touches. Part of the Shaman epic.

The butler and the maid each have two forms. Both spawn as either a robed dark elf or a vampire, and their placeholders differ — a deathly harbinger for Butler Syncall, a deathly usher for Maid Issis. The form does not appear to change the loot table, but it does change whether ordinary invisibility or invisibility versus undead will hide you from them.

05Dangers

Trains are the defining hazard, and the geometry makes them worse. Most trains originate deep in the castle, and on the way out every creature along the route joins. All of them funnel through the middle of the outer area between the tower and the lake on their way to the narrow corridor to the zone edge. The only shelter is behind the lake or behind the tower — or zoning.
The zone shuts down after a large train. Pulling from the line of returning monsters as they walk slowly back to the castle is itself lethal, and they loiter near the entrance for some time before returning. Zone in invisible if you can, because you cannot know what is waiting on the other side.
Invisibility is two spells here, and the right one changes twice a day. Ordinary invisibility works on the daytime population; invisibility versus undead works only on the undead. After the 20:00 swap the population you needed to hide from has changed. There is also a trap on the secret door at the jail that strips invisibility outright.
Sitting at a repop tick can aggro an entire room. Sitting gives level-1-equivalent aggro, and at 06:00 and 20:00 everything alive respawns while everything dead repops. Be standing at dawn and dusk.
Mynthi Davissi charms. She is a level 32 enchanter in the ballroom and the wiki recommends endure or resist magic before engaging. Note that the 28 July patch caps charm at 30 seconds — that limit applies to her charm on your group as much as to yours on anything else, which makes her materially less dangerous than classic guides suggest.
Deep castle trains can carry 500-plus damage nukes. The wiki is explicit that monsters capable of dealing 500 or more hit points with a single spell live in the interior. At the zone's 20–45 band that is a one-cast kill on most of the group.
The counterweight: nobody hunting the front need die. That is the wiki's own claim, and the reasoning holds — the zone edge is close, so a group with Spirit of Wolf up that zones at the first sign of trouble should not lose anyone. It requires that nobody tries to be a hero.

06Getting there

  • Reached through Lesser Faydark, at the western edge of its southern border. An ancient druid ring stands at the entrance to the zone.
  • Lesser Faydark is largely safe for anyone strong enough to fight in Mistmoore, but three camps are not: the Brownie camp to the north, the Shadowed Men camp to the east, and powerful dark elves in an abandoned camp along the southern edge.
  • Do not run the southern edge as a shortcut to the tunnel — that is where the dark elves have settled.
  • Lesser Faydark is the only adjacent zone. There is one way in and one way out, which is why the entry-corridor train problem has no alternative route.
  • Succor and evacuate drop you at −295, 123, the zone line to Lesser Faydark.
