Six hazards

Cautions

  • Trains They start deep in the castle, gather everything on the way out, and funnel between the tower and the lake into the one corridor to the zone line. Shelter is behind the lake, the tower, or the zone line. Deep trains carry 500-plus damage nukes — a one-cast kill at 20–45.
  • After a large train The returning line loiters near the entrance; pulling from it is lethal. Zone in invisible: you cannot see what waits.
  • Invisibility is two spells Ordinary invisibility covers the day population, invisibility versus undead the night, and the 20:00 swap changes which you need. The trap on the jail's secret door strips both.
  • Sitting at a repop tick Sitting gives level-1-equivalent aggro, and at 06:00 and 20:00 the whole population turns over. Be standing.
  • Mynthi Davissi charms The 28 July patch caps charm at 30 seconds, hers included, which makes her less dangerous than classic guides suggest.
  • The approach Lesser Faydark is safe enough for anyone fighting here, except three camps: Brownies north, Shadowed Men east, dark elves along the southern edge. One way in, one way out.
