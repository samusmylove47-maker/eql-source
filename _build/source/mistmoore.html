01Why you come here

  • The population changes at dawn and dusk. Twice a day in game time, at 06:00 and 20:00, the undead give way to dark elves and back. What you can camp depends on the clock.
    • The front is survivable long before the castle is.

06Dangers

Trains are the defining hazard, and the geometry makes them worse. They start deep in the castle, collect everything on the way out, and funnel between the tower and the lake into the one corridor to the zone edge. Shelter is behind the lake, behind the tower, or through the zone line.
The zone shuts down after a large train. The returning line loiters near the entrance, and pulling from it is lethal. Zone in invisible — you cannot see what is waiting on the other side.
Invisibility is two spells here, and the right one changes twice a day. Ordinary invisibility works on the daytime population; invisibility versus undead works only on the undead. After the 20:00 swap the population you needed to hide from has changed. There is also a trap on the secret door at the jail that strips invisibility outright.
Sitting at a repop tick can aggro an entire room. Sitting gives level-1-equivalent aggro, and at 06:00 and 20:00 everything alive respawns while everything dead repops. Be standing at dawn and dusk.
Mynthi Davissi charms. Note that the 28 July patch caps charm at 30 seconds — that limit applies to her charm on your group as much as to yours on anything else, which makes her materially less dangerous than classic guides suggest.
Deep castle trains can carry 500-plus damage nukes. At the zone's 20–45 band that is a one-cast kill on most of the group.

07Getting there

  • Reached through Lesser Faydark, at the western edge of its southern border.
  • Lesser Faydark is largely safe for anyone strong enough to fight in Mistmoore, but three camps are not: the Brownie camp to the north, the Shadowed Men camp to the east, and powerful dark elves in an abandoned camp along the southern edge.
  • One way in and one way out, which is why the entry-corridor train problem has no alternative route.
