Five hazards

Cautions

  • Trains They start deep and funnel between the tower and the lake into the one corridor out. Shelter behind the lake, the tower or the zone line. Deep ones carry 500-plus nukes.
  • After a large train The returning line loiters near the entrance and pulling from it is lethal. Zone in invisible.
  • Invisibility is two spells Ordinary covers the day population, versus-undead the night, and the 20:00 swap changes which you need. The jail's secret door strips both.
  • Sitting at a repop tick Sitting gives level-1-equivalent aggro, and at 06:00 and 20:00 the population changes.
  • The approach Lesser Faydark is safe except Brownies north, Shadowed Men east, dark elves south.
