Plane of Hate

12

A city on three levels, and minibosses that each upgrade one trash type

A city of some thirty buildings on three usable levels, monsters at 48 and up behind a hard level gate at 46. Come for Head, Arms, Wrist and Legs — the four planar slots the Plane of Fear does not have — and for the minibosses, each the upgraded form of one ordinary mob. The city is the difficulty, not the mobs: every street looks the same in every direction. Innoruuk and the Maestro exist only in the raid instance, and there is no ordinary way out.

Four hazards

Cautions

  • The city is the difficulty Around thirty buildings on a ground floor that looks the same in every direction, with a second level above it. You will lose the way back before you lose a fight.
  • The object you touch decides the zone Sword for the public instance, voidling for the raid instance. Pick the sword and the two raid bosses are not there at all.
  • There is no ordinary way out Gate, Origin, a wizard or druid port, or die. Succor returns you to the zone-in at −375, −354.
  • Invisibility is unreliable Many mobs see through invisibility and through invisibility versus undead, and Innoruuk sees rogue hide. T5

What the difficulty tiers change, measured →

Both published respawn timers describe something that no longer happens — 7 days for Innoruuk, 3 for the Maestro, the open-world spawn the 16 June patch removed. Access is governed instead by the weekly bonus-loot lockout, which resets Tuesdays at 8AM PST; a kill made while locked still returns one guaranteed drop from the boss’s unique table (28 July patch note). Instances cost no experience — the 23 June patch note raised instanced Hate, Fear and Sky to match the open world — and drops match the tier you are in, with a diminishing chance to roll higher (29 July hotfix), which also governs what a merge is worth. One player reports that D0 through D4 each carry their own lockout, which would mean a first clear at D0 costs nothing at any higher tier. Reported 10 August 2026 and not stated either way in the patch notes.

Armour table T3 — eqlwiki, maintained by editor Mazirian through 22 July 2026. It has outside support: a creator farming here in August killed a revultant rat and a haunted chest together and got a Magician wrist piece, and the table puts the rat at Wrist, Group 2, which is where the Apothic set sits.

Three mob types split by gender, and they split in opposite directions. A female forsaken revenant drops Group 1 arms and a male drops Group 2. The elite dragoon and Innoruuk’s Chosen run the other way round. If you are farming a slot and getting the wrong group, check the gender before you conclude the table is wrong.

Miniboss table T5 — imported, except that Lord of Loathing is named in the 2 June 2026 patch note among bosses given Solo Raid balance changes, alongside Innoruuk, Cazic-Thule and the Maestro. You will not meet all ten in one run: a player clearing the zone repeatedly, 13 July 2026, reports them spawning in varying places and as alternates — a Lord of Ire one time, a Lord of Loathing the next. Read the table as the pool, not the roster of a single clear.

Two things in the source do not line up, flagged here rather than fixed. a scorn banshee is filed as “Group a” in one table and Group 1 in another while its miniboss drops a Group 2 set, and the same set is called both Imbrued Armor and Imbrued Platemail Armor on one page.

A haunted chest — resolved, by two sources agreeing

The imported page says a haunted chest drops any planar armour, including Fear-only pieces. T5 Two independent Legends-era accounts say it drops Lustrous Russet and nothing else: a guild loot guide of 18 June 2026, and a creator farming them for a session on 9 August 2026 — “All they drop is the russet gear.” Two reports, seven weeks apart, one post-launch, neither citing the other. The imported line is classic and this is settled against it. The mirror holds in the Plane of Fear, where phoboplasms drop Midnight Clad. A logged session of chest kills would raise it from settled to measured.
