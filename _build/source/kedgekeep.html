01Why you come here

  • ZEM 139, or 185%. That is @@ZEM_RANK@@, and far ahead of the planes. The 23 June patch note singles the zone out: “Kedge has retained its original modifier for those willing to brave the depths.” That is the developers saying the rate is the reward for the inconvenience.
  • Four class epics converge on one boss. Phinigel Autropos carries components for the Wizard, Bard, Rogue and Magician epics. No other single surveyed mob gates four.
  • Blunt and slashing weapons appear to work here. On Live EverQuest and Project 1999 the underwater penalty made piercing the only sensible choice. A player reports landing normal hits with a sword. Section 02.
  • You cannot come here without water breathing. Not a difficulty setting — the whole dungeon is underwater and a corpse recovery without it is close to impossible.

02The weapon rule, and why it is the headline

This is the one claim on the page that came from a player rather than from a wiki, and it is the reason to read the zone afresh.

Classic EverQuest penalised slashing and blunt weapons underwater, which made piercing the default in water zones and shaped every inherited opinion about what to bring here. That rule has never been sourced to a citable record, and this page says so rather than repeating it as established.

In Legends it appears not to apply. A player fighting here on 9 August 2026 reports landing normal hits with a sword.

The eqlwiki Kedge Keep page carries {{Classic Era}}, discusses breathing at length, and says nothing whatever about weapon types underwater — it neither confirms nor denies. So the only Legends-era evidence points one way, and it is one player’s report rather than a parsed log.

What would settle it: one combat log line showing a slashing or blunt weapon landing normal damage while submerged. If a penalty exists it should show as a visible drop in average damage against the same mob type fought on land. Until then this stays an open question on the register, credited on the credits page.

03What the sources are

Better than the planes, and still mostly imported.

The eqlwiki Kedge Keep page was created on 21 August 2024 by P99Wiki>Cconley717 — a Project 1999 import from nearly two years before EverQuest Legends existed. The Phinigel Autropos page carries {{Classic Era}} and a single undifferentiated hit-point figure.

One edit stands out. On 8 August 2026, after launch, editor Mandlz added a Raid Instance section describing Phinigel as a Legends raid encounter entered by hailing a voidling, with solo and multiplayer instances sharing a lockout. That is genuinely current material and it is the most recent Legends-era editing found on any surveyed zone. It carries no edit comment and states no method, so it is T3 rather than tier 2 — but it is not classic text.

The section also leaves two of its own fields as TBD: level of monsters and types of monsters in the raid instance. Neither is filled in here either.

04Getting in, and staying alive in it

  1. Dagnor’s Cauldron, southern tip of the lake swim down a broken entrance in the depths
  2. Water breathing first item, spell or bard song there is no version of this zone without it
  3. Succor puts you at 14, 100 a caged room a long way from the exit — know this before you press it

Succor here is not a way out. On most surveyed zones, evacuate drops you at or near the zone line. The published succor point for Kedge is a caged room distant from the exit, so evacuating solves the fight you are losing and starts a different problem. T5

05The shape of the zone, and what the plan can and cannot show

Six hundred units of vertical range across three separated bands. The source page says the zone “extends vertically as well as horizontally”, and the mesh extraction agrees with it independently — which is worth saying, because the two come from completely different places.

The caveat, and it is a real one. The floor plans draw walkable floor — the surfaces a standing character could stand on. Kedge Keep is a zone you swim through. Large parts of where you can actually go are open water with no floor beneath them, so this plan under-draws the navigable space more than any other on the site. Read it as the skeleton of the building, not as the map of where you may move.

Why the Fiery Avenger finding matters here more than anywhere

On 10 August 2026 a player completed every step of the paladin epic, final turn-in included, as a non-paladin. The reward is still flagged Class: PAL, so holding it and earning it have come apart.

Kedge Keep is where that question gets expensive. If the same is true of the four epics gated behind Phinigel, then who may run them is a far wider group than the class names suggest — and in a game where a character carries three classes at once, that is not an exotic edge case. That is one report, and one report does not generalise. Testing any one of these four would settle it, and this is the obvious place to test.

Access. Phinigel stopped spawning in the open world and in personal instances on 11 June 2026. He is reached by hailing a voidling, and the raid section notes that solo and multiplayer instances share a lockout — so running the solo version does not leave the group version free that week. T3
