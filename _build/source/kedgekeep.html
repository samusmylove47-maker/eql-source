Kedge Keep

13

Underwater from end to end — four class epics converge on one boss

A flooded keep under the lake at Dagnor’s Cauldron, levels 32 to 50. Come for the experience — ZEM 139 is @@ZEM_RANK@@ — and for Phinigel Autropos, who gates components for four class epics. Water breathing is not optional, and succor strands you in a caged room far from the exit. Little of the zone is measured — a short run at base difficulty, with no drops — but Phinigel himself is fought.

Four hazards

Cautions

  • No water breathing, no zone Item, spell or bard song, before you swim down. A corpse recovery without it is close to impossible.
  • Succor is not a way out Elsewhere evacuate lands you at the zone line. Here it lands you at 14, 100 — a caged room distant from the exit. T5
  • Phinigel takes your options away Summons, uncharmable, unfearable, unmezzable, sees invisible, immune to run-speed changes, and a belly caster. T5
  • The piercing-only rule is inherited, not sourced Classic EverQuest penalised slashing and blunt underwater and no citable record carries that rule. A player fighting here on 9 August 2026 landed normal hits with a sword.

Who may run an epic is open, and this is where it gets expensive. On 10 August 2026 a player completed every step of the paladin epic, final turn-in included, as a non-paladin; the reward is still flagged Class: PAL. If that holds for the four gated here, far more characters may run them than the class names suggest. One report does not generalise, and testing any one of these four would settle it.

Every other named drops one common and one rare piece. Those are listed in the roster above rather than repeated here.
