Splitpaw Lair

02

One dungeon that carries a character from 25 to the low 40s

One long climb north, 25 at the door to the low 40s at the top, and no other zone in this series carries a character that far without leaving. The gnolls run a full class hierarchy, and their names announce what they will do to you before you pull. Snare or root is not optional: they flee, they carry a lot of hit points and they cover ground fast. No session here has been logged, so everything below is read rather than measured.

Key to the plan

Where you are

You enter at the bottom and the deepest named sits at the top. Your Y is your depth gauge — everything gets harder as it rises, and a falling Y means you are heading out.

Three camps
Double Doors, low-to-mid 30s: the entrance up to the double doors and the rooms behind them. Judges and Executioner, mid 30s to low 40s: on through the next room, over the bridge, round the U-shaped room and over a second bridge, for near-continuous pulls and three named. The Ishva Area, 40 and up: off the first bridge, drop down, right, through a door on the left and up the ramp to the pond, then the underwater passage — thirteen mobs inside, and another area within swimming distance

Nothing in this zone sees invisibility. Invis and walk straight to the camp you want, and stay out of the safe room’s doorway: it is the recovery point for the whole upper half. Getting in, take Circle of North Karana (druid, group) or North Karana Portal (wizard, group) and run south; Evacuate: North Karana comes a level later and is an escape, not travel. It is a long walk from Qeynos or Highpass, so bind in Arena, or better, at the camp.

Reading a gnoll

The prefix is the class and the middle word is the power rank, so the name is the con. Power runs Mas → Mal → Val, weakest to strongest: a Tesch Mas Gnoll is an entry-band warrior and a Rosch Val Gnoll is a deep-band mage.

Tesch
Warrior. Hits hard, no surprises
Lteth
Rogue. Will backstab anything that lets it get behind
Nisch
Cleric or shaman. Heals its friends. Kill it first or interrupt — a Nisch in a pull turns a twenty-second fight into a two-minute one
Rosch
Caster. Mages, enchanters, necromancers. Pets and area damage

Four hazards

Cautions

  • Runners Snare or root is not optional here. Gnolls flee, they have a lot of hit points and they cover ground fast, so a runner pulls in half a room or comes back with a healer.
  • Nothing walks alone From the front door, nearly every gnoll is paired. You need to be able to take two at once just to walk in.
  • Walls do not block social aggro Mobs know you are fighting their friends through solid rock. It bites hardest at the small room past the first bridge, where two tougher gnolls sit around the corner and through a gate. If you hear chains rattling, turn and run.
  • The prison cells Step inside and the door shuts with the lever on the outside. The main prison hall does not do this — that door can be walked through freely.

Otherwise Splitpaw is a fair zone: mini-trains rather than Blackburrow-scale disasters, wanderers rare and solitary enough to learn, rare spawns in their own pockets away from traffic. One roamer is worth naming, on the bridge at the Double Doors camp. Do not pull it and the camp stays clean.
