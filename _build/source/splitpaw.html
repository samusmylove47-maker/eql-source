02Reading a gnoll

Splitpaw shares something with only one other zone in Norrath: the gnolls have a full class hierarchy, and their names announce what they will do to you before you pull them.

Prefix

Tesch

Warrior. Straightforward. Hits hard, no surprises.

Prefix

Lteth

Rogue. Will backstab anything that lets it get behind.

Prefix

Nisch

Cleric / shaman. Heals its friends. Kill first or interrupt — a Nisch in a pull turns a twenty-second fight into a two-minute one.

Prefix

Rosch

Caster. Mages, enchanters, necromancers. Pets and area damage.

Power rank runs Mas → Mal → Val, weakest to strongest. So a Tesch Mas Gnoll is an entry-band warrior and a Rosch Val Gnoll is a deep-band mage. The name is the con.

03The three camps

Camp 1 · low-to-mid 30s

Double Doors

Everything from the entrance up to the double doors, plus the rooms directly behind them. The lowest camp and the natural starting point.

Do not pull the roamer on the bridge. That one mob is the difference between a clean camp and a nasty train.

Camp 2 · mid 30s to low 40s

Judges & Executioner

Through the double doors, through the next room, across the bridge, around the U-shaped room, across a second bridge. Near-continuous pulls from the executioner room, the judges and the Vals on the side, plus three named in the area.

Nothing in this zone sees invisibility. Invis and walk straight here.

Camp 3 · 40 and up

The Ishva Area

Past the double doors to the first bridge, drop down, go right. A door on the left, in and up the ramp until a pond appears. Take the underwater passage.

Thirteen mobs inside plus another area within swimming distance. The highest camp in the dungeon.

There is a small safe room at 1150, −180, directly north of Nisch Val Torash Mashk. Stay out of the doorway and it is safe to medate there — the recovery point for the whole upper half of the zone.

Loot every scroll. Scroll drops here turn in for experience in South Qeynos, and the turn-ins stay worth doing into the high 40s. The wiki's author calls this the zone's main benefit, ahead of the gear. Volume 1 doesn't drop inside the zone, but volumes 2–4 do.

06Dangers

Snare or root is not optional here. Gnolls flee, they have a lot of hit points, and they cover ground fast — a runner will pull in half a room, or come back with a healer. The wiki is explicit that snaring or rooting is required in this dungeon.
Almost nothing is solo. Starting at the entrance, nearly every gnoll is paired. You need to be able to take on two at once just to walk in the front door.
Walls don't block social aggro. Mobs know you're fighting their friends through solid rock. This bites hardest at the small room past the first bridge: the gnolls there are manageable, but around the corner and through a gate sit two tougher ones who come running. If you hear chains rattling, turn and run.
The prison cells are a trap. Step inside and the door shuts with the lever on the outside. The main prison hall does not do this — that door can be walked through freely.

Beyond that, Splitpaw is a fair zone. Trains here are mini-trains rather than Blackburrow-scale disasters, wandering mobs are rare and usually solitary enough to learn, and the rare spawns sit in their own pockets away from traffic.

07Getting there

  • Enter Southern Plains of Karana and travel to the centre. Look for three bloody claws sticking up out of the earth — gnoll legend says their god set his foot here and the surface markings are the footprint. Pass between the claws; in what would be the centre of the paw is a small dark hole facing them. It is easy to walk straight over, so slow down.
  • Circle of North Karana (Druid, group) or North Karana Portal (Wizard, group), then run south.
  • Evacuate: North Karana arrives one level after the Wizard portal and is a group escape rather than a travel spell.
  • It is a long walk from Qeynos or Highpass Hold. Binding in Arena helps; binding at the camp inside the zone helps more.
  • Succor and evacuate drop you at −122, 63, the zone line back to South Karana.
