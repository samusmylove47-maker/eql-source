What tonight
costs you later.

Faction moves while you are not looking. You clear a zone for six hours and find out afterwards that an unlock you had not started is now expensive, or that a vendor has stopped speaking to you. This checks that before you commit the evening.

65 factions1 zone measured 25 quest steps16 races

Read the coverage before you trust a silence. The faction movement here is measured from our own combat logs, so it covers only zones we have actually played and counted: The Castle of Mistmoore. Every other zone on this site is not measured, and this page will tell you so rather than return an empty result that looks like good news. What a faction is for — which races need it, which quest steps raise it — comes from the race unlock work and covers everything we hold. An absent zone means we have not looked, never that it is safe.

Where we cannot answer

These zones have plates but no faction measurement: Najena, Lair of the Splitpaw, Crushbone, Befallen, Blackburrow, Lower Guk, Nagafen's Lair, The Hole, The Warrens. Faction data for them would come from a combat log of an hour in the zone — the same way the measured zone above was produced. Until then this page has nothing to say about them, and says nothing rather than guessing.
