Where this comes from

  • Unlock requirements, methods and faction values — Alanna's Race Unlock Guide on eqlwiki.com, revision 166686, last edited 5 August 2026. That is the definitive deep-dive; every quantity, NPC, zone and per-turn-in faction figure below traces to it. For full dialogue, alternative methods and step-by-step detail, read it directly.
  • Race / primary class matrix, alignments, racials and starting cities — eqlwiki.com/Character_Races, revision 163936, last edited 28 July 2026 (launch day). That page carries a general "needs updating" banner, but the class lists were revised at launch and include Legends-specific changes from classic EverQuest — Kerran can take Druid, Wood Elf cannot take Beastlord. Treat the class lists as current and the surrounding prose as older.
  • Unlock and lock mechanics — eqlwiki.com Newbie Guide, revision 158978, last edited 19 July 2026. Source of the level 11 lock, the free level-50 primary class unlock, and the rule that unlocking a primary class makes it selectable by any race.
  • Race Unlock Tokens — both the Newbie Guide and Alanna's guide describe them as a store purchase that bypasses the faction work. The Newbie Guide still carries a "not yet implemented" note dated 19 July; players have since reported buying and using them. Treat availability as current and the wiki note as stale. No price is published on either wiki page, so this tool does not quote one.
  • Time and kill counts are Alanna's figures where she gives them and clearly-marked estimates where she does not. Vendor prices are not published per item, so purchase cost is shown as quantities to buy rather than a plat total.
