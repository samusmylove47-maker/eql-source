# The 9 August 2026 external audit — rulings and work plan

An outside review compared eqlsource against eqlwiki, eqlegendstools and
eqltools and returned 19 findings. Every one was checked against the site, the
data, or the live wiki before being accepted. **This file is the ruling on each,
and the plan of work.** It exists so that a later session does not re-litigate
settled questions or act on the findings that were checked and found wrong.

**Read this before acting on anything in the audit document.** Three of its
findings are wrong, and two of those would make the site *less* accurate if
applied as written.

---

## How to use this file

- **Ruling** is decided. Do not reopen without new evidence.
- **Status** is `todo`, `doing`, `done (PR #n)`, or `deferred`.
- A batch maps to one pull request. The human merges; nothing publishes without
  that.

### 1.2 Accepted, but the audit's diagnosis is wrong — fix differently

#### A — Najena respawn

**The audit says:** publish 19:02 open-world and 4:15 instanced as a pair;
4:50 matches neither.

**Rejected as diagnosed.** Those two figures are on the **Najena (NPC)** page and
are *that boss's* respawn. `4:50` is the **zone** spawn timer from the zone
page's infobox. Different quantities. Verified 9 Aug 2026 by reading both pages
raw.

**The real defect is worse.** The zone page carries `{{Classic Era}}`, and its
prose reads *"Respawn is 19 minutes for most mobs."* Our plate prints **19:00**
in the zone-respawn slot and argues it is "current, not a ceiling" — so **we
published a Tier 5 import in a structured field and reasoned about its
currency.** The index card's 4:50 is correct and comes from the T2 infobox.

**Fix:** zone respawn is 4:50, sourced to the infobox. Per-mob spawn cycles are
a separate field, never the zone figure. Where a source gives an open-world and
an instanced value, carry both — that dimension is real and nobody on the site
had modelled it. Write it up as a Correction.

#### B — Placeholders in revamped dungeons

**The audit says:** eqlwiki `Category:Named Mobs` states revamped dungeons have
no placeholders, so the roster percentages are wrong and should come down.

**Accepted as a problem, rejected as a fix.** Applying the provenance test to
that category page, 9 Aug 2026:

- Oldest revision **23 February 2019, `P99Wiki>Loramin`** — a Project 1999
  import, so Tier 5 by origin.
- Tagged `{{Delete}}` on 24 May 2026, reverted the same day.
- The placeholder sentence was added **10 July 2026 by `Caliente`**, revision
  155553, comment *"added a note about revamped dungeons not spawning
  placeholders"* — **18 days before EverQuest Legends launched.** A pre-launch
  note, not a measurement.

**And the wiki contradicts itself.** Drelzna's own page still reads *"Her
placeholder is a necromancer that spawns in front of the chair in the middle of
the room."*

**And we contradict ourselves.** Our Najena plate already quotes the 23 June
patch note — Tier 1 — saying the revamp brought *"a striking lack of
placeholders for named mobs"*, and then publishes placeholder identities and
spawn percentages as planning data on the same page.

**Fix:** publish the conflict, with all four sources dated and tiered, and mark
the percentages disputed rather than deleting them. Then test it against our own
logs: **Befallen and Blackburrow are both on the revamped list and both are in
`assets/measured.json`.** If a named spawned every cycle there, Tier M settles a
question the wiki cannot. That is this site's whole thesis, and it is the best
piece of content available to us.

#### H — Faction impact checker

**The audit says:** Frogloks of Guk appears in both the Falls and the Rises
column for The Ruins of Old Guk — a pipeline bug rendering an impossible claim.

**Accepted. This was first ruled wrong, and that ruling was the error.** The
first check ran against the *search* surface, which has no columns, and
concluded the finding described a UI that did not exist. The zone cards further
down the same page do have Falls and Rises columns, and the finding was correct
there.

**The cause, found 9 Aug:** `moved_in` was keyed on `(faction, zone)` and kept
whichever delta was largest in absolute terms. In The Ruins of Old Guk the
measured deltas are −5 and +5 for the same faction, so both columns rendered
from the one surviving entry and both said **−5 a kill**.

**And the underlying data is not a bug — it is a real finding we were hiding.**
Killing living frogloks costs Frogloks of Guk 5; killing the undead ones pays 5.
`a froglok tsu shaman` −5, `a vis ghoul knight` +5. The zone is split between
the two and our own logs caught it. Keying on direction as well as zone makes
both columns true and names the mob driving each.

**Also accepted from the same finding:**

- Copy: **"6 zone measured"** and **"the measured zone above"**. Both confirmed.
- **Sample size unsignalled.** Kerra Isle rests on 2 kills, Mistmoore on 1,018,
  rendered identically under "measured". Anything under 50 now carries *small
  sample — treat as seen once, not as a rate*.
- **The empty Blackburrow card was real.** 42 kills, two empty columns. The
  cause: `faction_by_mob` is empty for every Blackburrow session, so nothing
  reached either column, while five factions plainly moved. An empty card claims
  we looked and found nothing. It now names them under *Seen moving, direction
  unknown* and says what would close it.

**The lesson, worth keeping:** a finding was dismissed because the wrong surface
was tested. Check the surface the finding names.

### 1.3 Rejected

#### C — Journeyman's Boots. **Our conclusion stands.**

The audit says our "expect them off Drelzna" conclusion contradicts a Tier 2
record it calls *"specific, dated, non-classic-sounding"*. That record reads:

> "Journeyman's Boots (Rare) formerly dropped from this mob before being made a
> quest on **October 13, 1999 on Live**."

It names 1999 and it names Live. It is classic EverQuest text, which CLAUDE.md
forbids presenting as Legends fact. Meanwhile the same page's **drop table lists
Journeyman's Boots (Rare) as current loot**. Our plate weighed the structured
table against a 1999 hedge and went with the table. That is the tier hierarchy
working exactly as designed.

**Do not "fix" this.** A future session reading the audit will be tempted to.

**But adopt what the check turned up:** Drelzna's wiki drop list carries **Dark
Elf Parts (Always)**, **Bronze Greaves (Rare)** and **Tentacle Whip (Rare)**,
and none of the three is on our plate.

#### P — "Zero search presence" as a Critical finding

The domain went live on 8 August. The audit is dated 9 August. Nothing is
indexed in a day; this is arithmetic, not a finding. **The crawlability work is
accepted** — canonicals, item and mob URLs, server-rendered index content — but
not the severity or the framing.

#### The central thesis — "eqlsource is downstream of eqlwiki, and that is the ceiling"

**Half wrong, and it is the half the audit itself calls the crown jewel.**

- `assets/zone-geometry.json` is extracted from the game's own `.s3d` mesh
  archives by `_build/geometry.py`. Not from anyone's wiki. It caught six
  Najena coordinates the wiki has wrong.
- `assets/measured.json` is first-hand combat log data — Tier M — and produced
  the two findings nobody else in the ecosystem has: the difficulty tier names
  read from the zone line, and two class kits on *trash* at D1.

Those are the two places we are upstream of everyone. The strategic
recommendations built on the ceiling claim (the provenance layer, log
ingestion) are still good and are accepted below — but as *amplifiers of an
existing edge*, not as compensation for not having one.

#### Minor corrections to the audit's own facts

- "Learn is two pages" — there are three: `difficulty`, `deity`, `raid-access`.
- "eqlsource links to eqltools twice" — four times. Its point is stronger than
  it stated.

## 2. The plan

Ordered so that the fixes land first and the gate that prevents their recurrence
lands immediately after. **Accuracy outranks reach**, because testers arriving to
wrong numbers is worse than testers not arriving at all.

### Batch 1 — Consistency. No judgement calls. **Done.**

1. `E` — gaps section renders from the same data as the verification counters.
2. `F` — the 208 becomes a printed count.
3. `G` — one tracker count, printed; footer nav generated from the tool list so
   the faction checker cannot be orphaned again.
4. `I` — page metadata may not contain a figure the body tiers below T3.
5. `D` — a withheld coordinate is withheld everywhere, with its reason, not just
   from the plot.
6. `J` — one session count, printed; drop the internal path from reader-facing
   copy.
7. `H` copy — plural agreement, and sample size shown with every measured figure.

### Batch 2 — The build-time gate. **Done.**

Extend `scripts/check.py` so batch 1's class of fault cannot return:

- No count may differ across pages when both derive from the same fact.
- A coordinate listed in `WITHHELD` may not appear in any rendered table.
- A `meta`/`og:description` may not contain a number the body marks T3 or below.
- Every zone's `verify_level` must agree with the gates recorded in
  `verify_gate`.
- Every tool in the tools index must appear in the footer nav.

This is the audit's best structural recommendation and the site's actual cure.
The standard has been enforced by the author's attention; it needs to be
enforced by the build.

**Shipped in `scripts/gate.py`, with one lesson worth keeping.** While writing
it, check 5 was dead: its regex held two literal backspace bytes where `` was
meant, so it compiled, matched nothing, and reported "All checks passed" exactly
as it does when the site is clean. **A dead check is indistinguishable from a
passing one.** So `scripts/gate_selftest.py` mutates the real tree with each of
the six faults that actually shipped and asserts every one is caught. Run it
after touching the gate.

### Batch 3 — Accuracy. → PR after batch 2

8. `A` — respawn model: zone timer separate from per-mob cycle, open-world and
   instanced carried as a pair where the source gives one, all ten re-derived.
9. `B` — publish the placeholder conflict with four dated, tiered sources; mark
   the percentages disputed; test against Befallen and Blackburrow logs.
10. Drelzna gains Dark Elf Parts, Bronze Greaves and Tentacle Whip.

### Batch 4 — Reach. → PR after batch 3

11. `M` — contact route: a repository issue link and a per-page "report a
    correction" link that prefills zone and section. **No Discord and no email
    address without the human's decision — both are theirs to own, not mine.**
12. `L` — generated `og:image` per page type, built from the same data as the
    page.
13. `O` — canonical tags; internal links drop `.html`.
14. Lead with the geometry check on the home page. *"Every coordinate is
    validated against the game's own mesh files at build time — 176 of 176 land
    on walkable floor"* is the most defensible sentence available to us and it
    is currently a bullet on a sub-page.

### Deferred — real, and not this week

- Item and named-mob pages (`/items/…`, `/named/…`). 661 linkable URLs where
  there are none. The single highest-leverage reach change, and a large one.
- Quick-answer block at the top of every plate, above the provenance essay.
- Sticky table of contents on plates; site-wide search.
- The provenance layer: staleness dashboard, contradiction registry, automated
  classic-import detection. Accepted as direction — it is the position where
  being downstream is an advantage.
- Log ingestion as a browser tool. Accepted as direction. It attacks D3/D4 boss
  kits, respawn ceilings and unmeasured-zone faction data at once, and it is the
  only item that changes the growth curve.

## 3. What this audit got right that is worth keeping

The diagnosis — **excellent detection, no propagation** — is correct and was
independently confirmed the day before, when `sources.html` was found rendering
a hand-written change log eight entries behind the real one. Corrections here
get found, reasoned about at length, written up well, and then land in one place
instead of all the places.

The cure is not more care. It is fewer places where a fact can be typed.
