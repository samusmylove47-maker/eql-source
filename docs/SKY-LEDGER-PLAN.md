# Sky Ledger — findings and integration plan

**Written 17 August 2026.** The tool lives at `C:\Users\Lindsey\Desktop\ClaSkyApp`.
It is a separate repository with its own `CLAUDE.md`, `HANDOFF.md` and test suite.

---

## 1. What it is

A Plane of Sky class-unlock tracker that runs **as a real overlay on top of the
running game** (Electron), or **in a browser with no install** (a single 175 KB
`SkyLedger.html`). It reads the player's own combat log live, works out which of
the 95 Sky tests they can hand in right now, and tells them what the missing
pieces drop from.

- `npm start` runs the Electron shell; `npm run dist` builds a portable `.exe`.
- `SkyLedger.html` opens standalone in Chrome or Edge, no server, nothing uploaded.
- `node test.js` — **196 assertions, all passing** as of this review.
- Global hotkeys: `Ctrl+Shift+O` show/hide, `Ctrl+Shift+L` click-through.
- Continuous opacity slider 15–100%, position and state remembered between runs.

## 2. Why it belongs on eqlsource.com

**It already holds this site's standard, independently arrived at.** That is the
headline, and it is not a coincidence worth glossing over — the same person set
both standards.

- **It refuses to publish a drop rate it cannot measure.** No site publishes
  community Sky drop rates, so it prints what *your* log saw: drops over kills,
  with the sample size attached.
- **A dry streak is a ceiling, not a zero.** Nine kills with no drop reads
  `<28% · 0/9`, never `0%`. Zero successes in nine trials bounds the rate, it
  does not measure it — and printing `0%` would tell a player to stop farming
  something they should keep farming. This is the same reasoning as
  `damage_is_floor` on the raid pages.
- **It reports what it discarded.** A strip above the dock shows kills excluded,
  bag sales the client never itemised, and trades it refused to guess at. On a
  986,000-line log: 1,205 kills counted, 63 it could not place — and it says so.
  It hides itself when there is nothing to admit.
- **Its parsing is verified against the client's own string tables.**
  `CLIENT-TRUTH.md` records that `You have slain %1!`, `%1 has been slain by %2!`
  and `%1 died.` are ids 12113, 12114 and 12112 in `eqstr_us.txt`, read out of the
  install. That is the same class of evidence as `_build/geometry.py` reading the
  `.s3d` meshes.
- **It understands Legends instancing.** `The Plane of Sky 3 (Fused)` — the
  `<n> (<Tier>)` suffix that classic never had and that appears in no client
  string file.
- **`X died.` names no killer, so it is never counted as yours.** Ten lines in
  986,514, reported rather than guessed.

## 3. The thing no rival does

**A turn-in piece can only be spent once.** 29 of the 128 turn-in items are wanted
by more than one test, and every wind rune is wanted by several. Holding one Djinni
War Blade does not make three quests ready.

Sky Ledger treats held items as a **pool**, spends each unit on the test closest to
completion, and marks contested pieces with a warning showing how short you are.
Every other tracker — ours included — counts the same item three times and tells
you three quests are ready when one is.

**This is the reason to promote it above the plates.** It is not a nicer skin on
what we already publish; it is a correctness property our own tracker does not have.

## 4. What it replaces

`public/tools/plane-of-sky.html` — our current Sky tracker, generated by
`_build/build15.py` from `_build/source/eql-sky-tracker.html`, reading
`assets/sky.json`.

Sky Ledger supersedes it on every axis: live log reading, item contention,
measured drop bounds, overlay. **Retire ours rather than run both** — two Sky
trackers on one site is the "which do I use?" problem, and the older one is now
the wrong answer.

Note `assets/sky.json` (ours) and `sky.json` (theirs, 65 KB) are different
datasets. Theirs carries the dataset invariants the test suite asserts: 95 quests,
222 turn-in slots, 128 unique items, 29 contested, 16 classes.

## 5. Integration plan

### 5a. Landing page
Promote above the plate grid. The atlas moves down. The hero keeps
"Norrath, measured"; Sky Ledger becomes the first band under it — a feature block,
not a card in a row of equals.

What to say, in the site's voice and no longer than this:
- what it does in one line,
- the contention property (the thing nobody else has),
- the dry-streak bound (the thing that proves it thinks like this site),
- two doors: **run it in a browser** and **download the overlay**.

### 5b. Its own page
`/tools/sky-ledger.html`, generated. Screenshots, the hotkeys, the honest-limits
section from the README rendered in our own type, and the download.

### 5c. Hosting the app itself
The browser build is one self-contained 175 KB HTML file with no server. It can be
served **from this site** at e.g. `/app/sky-ledger.html` so a player can use it
without downloading anything. The Electron `.exe` needs a release download — GitHub
releases is the obvious host, and `_headers` already sets sane caching.

### 5d. Reskin
Their palette is their own. Ours is measured from the game's textures
(`assets/norrath-palette.json`) and documented in `docs/DESIGN.md` §11. The reskin
is a token swap plus the display face:
- warm umber grounds, brass line work, Cinzel for display, IBM Plex Mono for data;
- the provenance strip is *already* the cartographer's marginalia idiom — it just
  needs our colours;
- `SkyLedger.html` is **generated** by `build.py` from `src-shell.html`,
  `src-app.js`, `engine.js` and `sky.json`. **Edit the sources, never the
  concatenated file.**

### 5e. Attribution
The tool is the collaborator's own build. The site should say who made it, once,
without turning the page into a biography.

## 6. Open questions for the collaborator

1. **Does Sky Ledger's `sky.json` supersede our `assets/sky.json`?** If its data
   is better sourced, our Sky *page* should read from it too rather than keeping a
   second, weaker copy of the same facts.
2. **Where should the browser build live** — served from eqlsource.com, or linked
   to its own repo? Serving it makes the site the product; linking keeps the
   release cadence separate.
3. **Versioning.** If the app ships from this site, a stale cached copy is the
   same failure the stylesheet had. It needs a content hash in its URL.
4. **The other two tools** — the WeakAuras-equivalent and the sixtyupgrades
   equivalent — will want the same treatment. Worth deciding the pattern once
   here: a `/tools/` page each, promoted by capability rather than by novelty.
